*In plain English: A group of exchanges, brokers and market firms working on rules and plumbing so companies can put their own shares onchain, with holders keeping the same rights as normal shareholders.*

Bullish and Equiniti formed the Issuer Sponsored Token Coalition on 24 Sep 2026, a week after the SEC's [[concepts/innovation-exemption|Innovation Exemption]] ([Bullish](https://www.bullish.com/us/news-insights/leading-market-infrastructure-firms-form-coalition-to-advance-issuer-sponsored-tokenized-securities)). It backs the [[concepts/glossary#issuer-sponsored-token|issuer-sponsored token]] model, where the token is tied to the company's official shareholder register kept by its [[concepts/glossary#transfer-agent|transfer agent]] ([Bullish](https://www.bullish.com/us/news-insights/leading-market-infrastructure-firms-form-coalition-to-advance-issuer-sponsored-tokenized-securities)).

## Members
- **Founding (24 Sep 2026):** Bullish and Equiniti, with Alpaca, Apex Fintech Solutions and DriveWealth ([Bullish](https://www.bullish.com/us/news-insights/leading-market-infrastructure-firms-form-coalition-to-advance-issuer-sponsored-tokenized-securities)).
- **Joined 26 Oct 2026:** Qube Research & Technologies (QRT), [[infrastructure/vertalo|Vertalo]] and [[infrastructure/zerohash|zerohash]] ([Bullish](https://www.bullish.com/us/news-insights/issuer-sponsored-token-coalition-gains-momentum-as-qrt-vertalo-and-zerohash-join)).

Membership is open, non-exclusive and non-binding: joining does not commit a firm to issue, list, provide liquidity or support any product ([Bullish](https://www.bullish.com/us/news-insights/leading-market-infrastructure-firms-form-coalition-to-advance-issuer-sponsored-tokenized-securities)).

## Focus areas
1. Keep issuer and shareholder rights, including [[concepts/glossary#corporate-action|corporate-action]] entitlements.
2. Interoperability across traditional clearing and settlement, blockchains, [[concepts/glossary#wrapper|wrapped]]-token models and emerging [[concepts/glossary#entitlement-token|entitlement-token]] frameworks.
3. Infrastructure for issuance, transfer, settlement, custody and trading.
4. An open ecosystem of issuers, brokers, exchanges, transfer agents, custodians and market makers.

([Bullish](https://www.bullish.com/us/news-insights/leading-market-infrastructure-firms-form-coalition-to-advance-issuer-sponsored-tokenized-securities))

## What's next
Members were due to meet issuers at the New York Stock Exchange on 27 Oct 2026 ([Bullish](https://www.bullish.com/us/news-insights/issuer-sponsored-token-coalition-gains-momentum-as-qrt-vertalo-and-zerohash-join)). More members and initiatives were promised "in the coming weeks" ([Bullish](https://www.bullish.com/us/news-insights/leading-market-infrastructure-firms-form-coalition-to-advance-issuer-sponsored-tokenized-securities)).
