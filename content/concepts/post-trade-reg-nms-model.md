In this model the security does not change: tokenized and traditional shares share one order book, priority, CUSIP, symbol and rights, and settle through DTC's tokenization pilot ([SEC Nasdaq approval](https://www.sec.gov/files/rules/sro/nasdaq/2026/34-105047.pdf)). Components:
- **DTC Tokenized Entitlements** under the 11 Dec 2025 no-action letter ([[infrastructure/dtcc-tokenization-pilot|DTCC]]).
- **Exchange rules**: [[platforms-venues/nasdaq-tokenized-securities|Nasdaq]] (approved 18 Mar 2026), [[platforms-venues/nyse-ice-tokenized-platform|NYSE]].
Contrast with the [[concepts/innovation-exemption|onchain-venue model]] (TSVs).
