holders: About 41% of tokenized stocks by value sit with Binance's bStocks, most of it in Binance's own wallets, per a third-party estimate.

- Bitquery, a blockchain data firm, estimates \$3.2B of tokenized stocks outstanding on 31 Oct 2026 across six issuers, up from \$585M at end-2025. This is a third-party estimate, not an issuer or regulator figure ([Bitquery](https://bitquery.io/investigations/tokenized-stocks-market-size)). See [[topics/who-can-buy|Who can buy]].
