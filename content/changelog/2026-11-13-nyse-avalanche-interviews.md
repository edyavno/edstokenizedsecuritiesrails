- An Avalanche Originals interview with Michael Blaugrund of the New York Stock Exchange, uploaded 9 Nov 2026, is now on the [[platforms-venues/nyse-ice-tokenized-platform#settlement-ledger-avalanche|NYSE / ICE tokenized securities]] page, using Avalanche's official transcript. Blaugrund described Pillar as NYSE's matching system, said post-trade still runs on mainframes, and said the planned platform is meant to allow 24/7 trading, fractional share orders, dollar-denominated orders, settlement versus stablecoin, and immediate reuse of sale proceeds ([Avalanche Originals](https://www.avalanche.com/originals/the-invisible-layer-of-global-financial-infrastructure/), raw/304).
- Dennis Lee's line about "picking the right technology for each job" is now also on that page, from his 9 Nov statement ([CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/business/2026/11/09/nyse-picks-avalanche-to-settle-tokenized-stocks-amid-wall-street-s-blockchain-race), already raw/215). John Nahas of Ava Labs said NYSE spent a year testing Avalanche ([Cointelegraph](https://cointelegraph.com/interview/ava-labs-nyse-general-purpose-chains), 10 Nov, raw/305; also to [Paul Barron](https://www.paulbarronnetwork.com/network-update/nyse-chooses-avalanche-for-tokenized-equities-interview-ava-labs-john-nahas), 9 Nov, raw/306). That duration is Ava Labs' account; NYSE has not published a testing timeline.
- Nahas also told Paul Barron that "exclusivity" means NYSE is building its own Avalanche layer-1 as the backbone of the 24/7 ATS, and described a general AVAX burn per L1 validator while declining NYSE-specific token operations. Official NYSE and ICE materials do not say that, and Avalanche's own [NYSE page](https://www.avalanche.com/nyse/) (raw/307) defines an L1 only in general. Those points are attributed to Ava Labs. No launch date was added; Nahas left timing to NYSE and its regulators.
- The split-layer example in [[build/trading-reference-architecture#how-venues-match-and-settle|How venues match and settle]] now cites Blaugrund for Pillar and attributes the dedicated-L1 description to Ava Labs. The catalog one-liner, which had been cut off at "Sep", is completed.
