- New page: [[platforms-venues/aave|Aave]], covering the V4 Equities Hub on Base and Aave Horizon on Ethereum.
- Aave Labs said the Equities Hub was activated on 25 Sep 2026 with seven Coinbase tokens (AAPLc, AMZNc, GOOGLc, METAc, MSFTc, NVDAc, TSLAc) as collateral to borrow USDC, and that access is limited to eligible jurisdictions outside the U.S. ([Aave](https://aave.com/blog/coinbase-tokenized-stocks); [Aave Labs 180-day report](https://governance.aave.com/t/aave-labs-180-day-report/26342)). The tokens are collateral-only. Prices use Chainlink 24/5 feeds that hold the last print over weekends and U.S. holidays ([Aave](https://aave.com/blog/coinbase-tokenized-stocks)).
- SPCXc is treated as a live Equities Hub reserve in LlamaRisk's 23 Nov 2026 collateral-factor ARFC (current factor 55%). Aave Labs posted a Direct-to-AIP listing on 15 Oct 2026 and said AIP #537 voting would start on 27 Oct; the exact execution date is not stated ([Direct-to-AIP, SPCXc](https://governance.aave.com/t/direct-to-aip-asset-listing-spcxc-to-the-aave-v4-equities-hub-on-base/25850); [ARFC, collateral-factor update](https://governance.aave.com/t/arfc-collateral-factor-update-for-coinbase-tokenized-equities-on-base/26482)).
- Open proposals as of 23 Nov 2026, not stated as executed: LlamaRisk on 18 Nov proposed adding NFLXc, ORCLc, PLTRc, AMDc, NOWc and MSTRc as collateral, and on 23 Nov proposed ANTc at a 45% collateral factor plus cuts to six of the eight live factors. A lender reply the same day asked to delay or stage ANTc until after lock-up releases starting 15 Dec 2026 ([ARFC, expand](https://governance.aave.com/t/arfc-expand-coinbase-tokenized-equities-on-base/26441); [ARFC, collateral-factor update](https://governance.aave.com/t/arfc-collateral-factor-update-for-coinbase-tokenized-equities-on-base/26482)).
- [[issuers/coinbase-tokenized-stocks|Coinbase tokenized stocks]], [[analyses/anthropic-ipo-tokenized-stock-use-case|the Anthropic use case]], [[infrastructure/chainlink-equity-feeds|Chainlink equity feeds]] and the [[build/trading-reference-architecture#off-hours-pricing-and-collateral|reference architecture]] are updated to match. Horizon is a separate permissioned RWA market; official Aave sources reviewed on 23 Nov 2026 do not list Securitize stock tokens, xStocks, Backpack or Ondo equities as Aave collateral. Aave Labs said a Snapshot approved HINC, a Securitize-administered credit fund, for Horizon; whether it was then activated is not stated ([Aave Horizon docs](https://aave.com/docs/aave-v3/horizon); [Aave Labs October 2026 update](https://governance.aave.com/t/al-development-update-october-2026/25979)).
