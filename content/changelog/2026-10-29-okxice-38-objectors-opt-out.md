- OKXICE TSV: 38 issuers have objected as of its 28 Oct notice, up from 22 on 23 Oct; new names include JPMorgan, AMD, Coca-Cola, Dell, Eli Lilly and Walmart. Still no trading start date ([OKXICE notice](https://www.okx.com/cdn/assets/okfe/web3-activities/okxicetsv/notices/OKXICE_Tokenized_Securities_Venue_Public_Notice_26.10.28.pdf)).
- Innovation Exemption: issuers have 30 calendar days from a venue's notice to object, directly to that venue; opt-outs are per venue with no blanket option. Nasdaq says it intends to opt itself out of TSVs ([Nasdaq FAQ](https://www.nasdaq.com/docs/listings/faq-sec-innovation-exemption)).
- Comment file 4-927: Better Markets calls the order the biggest rewrite of equity trading rules since Reg NMS and urges the SEC to withdraw it and use notice-and-comment rulemaking ([Better Markets](https://bettermarkets.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Better-Markets-Comment-Letter-SEC-Innovation-Exemption.pdf)).
