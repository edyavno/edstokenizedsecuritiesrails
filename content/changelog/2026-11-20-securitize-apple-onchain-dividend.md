- [[topics/corporate-actions-and-dividends|Corporate actions]] and [[issuers/securitize|Securitize]]: on 16 Nov 2026, Apple's pay date, Securitize paid Apple's \$0.27 dividend in USDC on Solana to holders of its Securitize Stocks Apple token, which Securitize described as its first onchain dividend on a tokenized public stock. Bitquery, which traced the transfers, reported a total of \$43.14, with about 93% going to the market maker ([Bitquery](https://bitquery.io/investigations/securitize-apple-dividend-onchain); [Carlos Domingo on X](https://x.com/carlosdomingo/status/2123822462194643349)).
