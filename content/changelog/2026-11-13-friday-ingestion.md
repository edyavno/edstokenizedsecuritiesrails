- Metrika's 3 Nov 2026 comment letter on the SEC transfer agent proposal (File S7-2026-30) is now recorded on the [[topics/regulation|regulation]], [[topics/custody|custody]] and [[build/trading-reference-architecture|reference architecture]] pages. It addresses keeping the master securityholder file on a public chain the transfer agent does not control. Comment letters are advocacy, not law. Network-incident figures in the letter are Metrika's monitoring data (raw/291).
- [[issuers/ondo-global-markets|Ondo]] describes an institutional in-kind conversion through Alpaca's Instant Tokenization Network that returns shares rather than cash, alongside the existing USDon/USDC mint and redeem path, and a planned Spout covered-call lending use of that route when Spout launches on Solana ([Ondo](https://ondo.finance/blog/ondo-stocks-as-collateral-on-spout), raw/292).
- New jurisdiction entries: Bermuda, where the Bermuda Monetary Authority intends to amend existing laws for asset tokenisation rather than create a separate regime (stakeholder letter, 6 Nov 2026, raw/293); and Japan, where the FSA's FY2026 study group is an agenda under an on-chain finance forum, not yet rules (raw/294). Ireland's November 2026 Feedback Statement to Discussion Paper 12 is noted in one line under the EU section (raw/295).
- The [[infrastructure/dtcc-tokenization-pilot|DTCC Tokenization Service]] is planned to connect with Solana, with DTC-tokenized assets expected there in the second half of 2027 ([DTCC](https://www.dtcc.com/press-releases/2026/dtcc-tokenization-service-to-connect-with-solana), raw/296).
- Securitize and Ethena Labs launched a 24/7 liquidity facility for BlackRock's BUIDL, with \$50 million of initial inventory and a stated maximum capacity of \$100 million ([Securitize](https://investors.securitize.io/news/news-details/2026/Securitize-and-Ethena-Launch-100-Million-Liquidity-Facility-for-BlackRocks-BUIDL/default.aspx), raw/297). This is a fund, not a stock token.
- [[issuers/xstocks-backed|xStocks]] now records a native OP Mainnet deployment of its catalog and a 11 Nov 2026 Payward release stating that all 40 DAX constituents are tokenized and available on 360X, a Deutsche Börse company, along with Xetra-Gold. Catalog and volume figures are quoted with each source's date, because the 11 Nov Payward releases and the 12 Nov 2026 Optimism blog give different counts (raw/298, raw/299).
- [[issuers/securitize|Securitize Stocks]] is now 25 equities, after a 2 Nov 2026 second wave of 13 names on Solana (raw/300).
- [[infrastructure/tzero|tZERO]] reports CFTC approval of tZERO DCM, LLC as a Designated Contract Market on 11 Nov 2026, following tZERO DCO, LLC's 16 Oct 2026 registration. This is a derivatives-market registration, not a tokenized-securities product launch (raw/301).
