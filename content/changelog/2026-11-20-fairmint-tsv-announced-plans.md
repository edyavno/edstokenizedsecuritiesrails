- [[infrastructure/fairmint|Fairmint]] and [[comparisons/trading-venue-architectures|Trading venue architectures]]: on 20 Nov 2026 Fairmint announced plans for Fairmint TSV LLC, a wholly owned subsidiary, to operate a tokenized securities venue for automated market makers trading issuer-sponsored shares recorded by Fairmint TA. Trading would start on Canton, with Ethereum planned next, and Tradecraft would be the first AMM. The venue has not begun trading, and as of 2:59 PM ET that day it did not appear on the SEC's list of filed TSV notices ([GlobeNewswire](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/11/20/3407521/0/en/fairmint-announces-plans-for-fairmint-tsv-a-venue-connecting-amms-to-issuer-sponsored-tokenized-shares-with-tradecraft-as-first-amm.html); [SEC](https://www.sec.gov/about/divisions-offices/division-trading-markets/innovation-exemption)).
