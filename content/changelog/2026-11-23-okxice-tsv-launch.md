- [[platforms-venues/okxice-tsv|OKXICE TSV]]: OKX said the venue opened at noon ET on 23 Nov 2026 for eligible U.S. customers, in the TSV tab of the OKX app, with 113 assets confirmed for a rolling rollout, including stocks, ETFs and ETPs. Confirmed issuers and asset managers are Better.com, BitGo, Circle, Coinbase, Direxion, eToro, Fidelity Investments, Robinhood, Securitize, Strategy, Strive and Vida Global ([OKX](https://www.okx.com/en-us/learn/okxice-tsv-launch-going-live)). The 20 Nov 2026 public notice says that at launch the venue makes available six Tokenized NMS Stocks, each paired with USDC and each tokenized by Securitize Markets, LLC: COIN, SECZ, MSTR, BTGO, HOOD and CRCL. The same notice still lists 67 objectors, unchanged from 5 Nov, and names the X Layer contracts (TSVRegistryV1, TSVSwapRouterV1, TSVVolumeLedgerV1, TSVLPManagerV1, TSVHookV1, PermissionsAdapterFactory, an SBT contract, and Uniswap v4's PoolManager) and pre-launch audits by OKX Technology Inc. (internal) and Certified Kernel Tech LLC (external) ([OKXICE notice, 20 Nov](https://www.okx.com/cdn/assets/okfe/web3-activities/okxicetsv/notices/OKXICE_Tokenized_Securities_Venue_Public_Notice_26.11.20.pdf)).
- [[build/trading-reference-architecture|Trading reference architecture]] and [[comparisons/trading-venue-architectures|Trading venue architectures]] are updated for the live status and the contract-by-contract X Layer design. [[issuers/securitize|Securitize]] is updated for the tokenizer role on the live venue, which remains distinct from Securitize Stocks on Solana.
