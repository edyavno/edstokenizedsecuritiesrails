holders: Korea will treat security tokens as securities from 4 Feb 2027, starting with institutional funds and bonds and some fractional products, with a KRW100m yearly cap for retail buyers on each OTC exchange.

- South Korea added as a jurisdiction. The FSC's roadmap (4 Sep 2026) sets three phases from Feb 2027 ([FSC](https://fsc.go.kr/eng/pr010101/87653)); its draft rules (1 Oct 2026, comments to 11 Nov) cover shared ledgers with KSD, issuer account managers and retail caps ([FSC](https://fsc.go.kr/eng/pr010101/87834)). See [[concepts/international|International regimes]], [[topics/rules-by-jurisdiction|Rules by jurisdiction]] and [[topics/regulation|Regulation]].
