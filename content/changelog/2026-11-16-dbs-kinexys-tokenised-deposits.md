- On 16 Nov 2026 DBS and Kinexys by J.P. Morgan launched a commercially ready interoperability solution for instant USD transfers between blockchain-based accounts in the United States and Singapore, including on weekends. This is the cash leg, not a tokenized security: it connects DBS Token Services with Kinexys's account-based permissioned tokenized-deposit network. The DBS release says the solution "leverages Partior for settlement orchestration," and that the two banks are founding shareholders of Partior. Ant International is the inaugural client. On-network transfers are described as 24/7/365; moving funds between traditional demand-deposit accounts and Blockchain Deposit Accounts still has a three-hour downtime every Saturday from 3–6 p.m. ET ([DBS release](https://www.dbs.com/newsroom/DBS_and_Kinexys_by_JP_Morgan_launch_industry_first_247_interoperability_solution_for_cross_border_tokenised_deposit_transfers)).
