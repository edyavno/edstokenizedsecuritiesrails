holders: A first large European public-sector bond settled on a blockchain platform in central bank money, a sign that wholesale settlement is moving from pilots to live use.

- On 26 Oct 2026 the ESM settled a €1bn digital benchmark bond through Pontes, with Clearstream's D7 and the Bundesbank as issuing and paying agent ([Deutsche Börse](https://www.deutsche-boerse.com/dbg-en/media/news-stories/press-releases/ESM-is-first-European-supranational-issuer-to-settle-DLT-based-bond-issuance-on-Eurosystem-s-Pontes-platform-with-Bundesbank-and-Clearstream-5660764)).
