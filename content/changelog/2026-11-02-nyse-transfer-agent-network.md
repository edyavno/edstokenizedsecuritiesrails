NYSE now has six transfer agent partners for issuer-sponsored tokens on its planned tokenized securities platform: Broadridge, Computershare, Equiniti and Superstate join Securitize and tZERO. Per NYSE, the first three serve almost 99% of the S&P 500. Broadridge will also run on-chain shareholder voting. The platform still needs regulatory approval ([Broadridge](https://www.broadridge-ir.com/news/news-details/2026/Broadridge-to-Bring-OnChain-Governance-and-Digital-Transfer-Agency-to-New-York-Stock-Exchange-Tokenized-Securities-Platform/default.aspx); [Crypto Briefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/nyse-expands-tokenization-push-with-firms-serving-nearly-99-of-sp-500/)).
