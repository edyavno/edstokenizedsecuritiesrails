- New analysis: [[analyses/okxice-tsv-first-24-hours|OKXICE TSV's first 24 hours]]. In the 24 hours after the noon ET launch on 23 Nov 2026 (through noon ET on 24 Nov), the six launch USDC pools recorded 3,314 trades worth \$2,546,330.59. Sells outnumbered buys. Circle (CRCL) and Robinhood (HOOD) made up about two-thirds of the dollars, and just over half the dollar volume traded outside regular U.S. market hours. Official end-of-day pool size on the 23 Nov EST calendar day was \$1,224,370.68. Figures are from the venue's public TSV API, pulled around 12:30 PM ET on 24 Nov 2026 ([transaction-list](https://web3.okx.com/api/v6/explorer/tsv/transaction-list), [daily-statistics](https://web3.okx.com/api/v6/explorer/tsv/daily-statistics), [asset-list](https://web3.okx.com/api/v6/explorer/tsv/asset-list); [API docs](https://web3.okx.com/onchainos/dev-docs/market/onchaindata-tsv-reference)), with public X Layer records for sending addresses ([OKLink](https://www.oklink.com/x-layer/address/0xc58d2bf7ad7cb023760e767e9050be3f15cad686)) and Yahoo Finance as a market-price reference. Why 992 trades happened before the announced noon launch, the number of end users, and an overnight reference price are unknown. [[platforms-venues/okxice-tsv|OKXICE TSV]] now links to the analysis and notes that the daily-statistics feed is populated and that the asset list still showed the same six launch pools.
