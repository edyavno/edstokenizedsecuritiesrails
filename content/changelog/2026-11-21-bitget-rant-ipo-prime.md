- New pages: [[issuers/reality|Reality]] (issuer of rANT, per Bitget) and [[platforms-venues/bitget|Bitget]] (IPO Prime subscription and rANT spot listing). Bitget says rANT "is not equivalent to directly holding Anthropic shares" and does not automatically confer shareholder status or voting rights ([Bitget](https://www.bitget.com/support/articles/12560603899381); [Bitget rANT page](https://www.bitget.com/price/anthropic-tokenized-stock-reality)). It names Reality as issuer and says each token "maps 1:1 to a share" ([Bitget IPO Prime](https://www.bitget.com/spotlight/ipo-prime)).
- Bitget said it received a final allocation of 153,774 Anthropic shares (VIP 137,494 at 11.26%; public 16,280 at 1.36%), per Bitget, with unallocated funds to be refunded by the stated UTC deadlines ([Bitget](https://www.bitget.com/support/articles/12560603901105)). It announced a 19 Nov rANT spot listing during US regular market hours; the retrieved articles do not confirm the first trade time ([Bitget](https://www.bitget.com/support/articles/12560603901016)).
- [[comparisons/implementations-by-model|Implementations by model]] adds an rANT row. [[analyses/anthropic-ipo-tokenized-stock-use-case|The Anthropic use case]] adds rANT to the legal-model and allocation sections, and three rows to the dated timeline (announcement, listing notice, allocation). The swimlane chart was drawn before this ingest; the table includes rANT.
