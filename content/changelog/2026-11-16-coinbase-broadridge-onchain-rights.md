- On 16 Nov 2026 Coinbase and Broadridge published a jointly developed open technical standard, Onchain Rights, for delivering shareholder communications to wallets holding tokenized shares, and said they submitted it as an EIP for review. Broadridge also launched a data oracle that gives participating firms aggregate evidence of distribution. The standard's own docs say the specifications "communicate the action" and do not establish legal rights, calculate entitlement, determine eligibility, or submit votes; onchain voting is the next phase. As of 16 Nov 2026 the EIP texts and the canonical JSON schema were not yet published on the project's site ([Broadridge / PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/coinbase-and-broadridge-publish-proposed-standard-for-onchain-shareholder-communications-broadridge-launches-data-oracle-302939893.html); [Onchain Rights docs](https://www.onchainrights.org/docs)).
