- IOSCO's 2026 monitoring report (FR/06/26) now replaces the 2025 report where the wiki describes how far tokenization has got. It calls tokenization "transitional and fragmented," separates "mirroring" tokens from tokens whose chain records ownership, and puts tokenized equities at about \$1.7B on 30 Jun 2026, citing RWA.xyz ([IOSCO](https://www.iosco.org/library/pubdocs/pdf/IOSCOPD832.pdf)). Updated: [[overview|Overview]], [[concepts/legal-taxonomy|Legal taxonomy]], [[topics/corporate-actions-and-dividends|Corporate actions]], [[concepts/international|International]], [[topics/who-can-buy|Who can buy]].
- Four senators asked SEC Chairman Atkins 13 questions about the [[concepts/innovation-exemption|Innovation Exemption]], on AML/KYC, token-level transfer restrictions and issuer opt-outs, and urged notice-and-comment rulemaking ([letter](https://www.alsobrooks.senate.gov/news/press-releases/alsobrooks-leads-colleagues-in-pushing-sec-for-answers-transparency-on-tokenized-stock-change/)). SEC Trading and Markets Director Jamie Selway, speaking for himself, said a permanent rule proposal has "got to be a 2027 event" ([video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mwAidpXpJJ4&t=4255s)). Coin Center's comment letter is added to the comment file ([Coin Center](https://www.sec.gov/comments/4-927/4927-1145899-4073428.pdf)).
- Talos, Marex and [[infrastructure/broadridge|Broadridge]] announced an RFQ, prime-brokerage and atomic-settlement workflow for financing DTCC-tokenized Treasuries beyond market hours; no go-live date was given ([Broadridge](https://www.broadridge.com/press-release/2026/broadridge-to-enable-institutional-trading-of-dtcc-tokenized-securities)). Updated: [[infrastructure/dtcc-tokenization-pilot|DTCC Tokenization Service]], [[topics/settlement-and-matching|Settlement & matching]], [[comparisons/trading-venue-architectures|Trading venue architectures]].
- [[topics/settlement-and-matching|Settlement & matching]] adds Fed Governor Waller's view that net and batch settlement will stay useful alongside atomic settlement ([Federal Reserve](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/speech/waller20261117a.htm)), the SIFMA and Deloitte digital-cash white paper ([SIFMA and Deloitte](https://www.sifma.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Digital-Cash-White-Paper-SIFMA-and-Deloitte.pdf)), and the MAS and Bundesbank Project Simbolon cross-ledger PvP proof of concept ([MAS](https://www.mas.gov.sg/-/media/mas-media-library/development/fintech/project-simbolon/project-simbolon-insights-note.pdf)). [[topics/regulation|Regulation]] notes the CFTC's 15 Dec 2026 roundtable on 24/7 trading ([CFTC](https://www.cftc.gov/PressRoom/PressReleases/9327-26)).
- [[issuers/superstate-opening-bell|Superstate]] records Invesco's IROXX, the first registered fund on FundOS, whose prospectus makes the token records plus an offchain identity register the official register ([IROXX prospectus, 485BPOS](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/205007/000110465926117813/tm2627770d1_485bpos.htm)), and Exposure on Solana, an announced design where only minting and burning are permissioned; no Exposure Token is live yet ([Superstate](https://superstate.com/newsroom/superstate-formation-and-corda-join-forces-to-launch-exposure-the-official-solana-rwa-launchpad)).
- Short items: [[standards/erc-3643|ERC-3643]] cross-chain compliance aligned with Swift standards ([ERC-3643 Association](https://www.erc3643.org/news/interoperable-compliance-for-on-chain-capital-markets-aligned-with-swift-standards)); HKMA and Swift on Project Ensemble ([HKMA](https://www.hkma.gov.hk/eng/news-and-media/press-releases/2026/11/20261116-4/)); [[issuers/wisdomtree|WisdomTree]] funds registered in Singapore ([Synthesys](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/synthesys-announces-collaboration-with-wisdomtree-following-registration-of-three-tokenized-funds-in-singapore-302940804.html)); Alpaca's own claim of more than \$10B in cumulative mint and redeem volume, not trading volume, noted on [[issuers/xstocks-backed|xStocks]] and [[comparisons/reported-trading-volume-by-venue|reported volumes]] ([The Block](https://www.theblock.co/news/business/2026-11-17-alpaca-says-tokenization-network-tops-10-billion-minting-redemption-volume-442667)); [[issuers/securitize|Securitize]]'s broker-dealer FINRA custody approval from its 10-Q ([Securitize 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2094496/000162828026073910/secz-20260930.htm)); a one-line CFTC DCM/DCO note on [[issuers/backpack|Backpack]] ([CFTC](https://www.cftc.gov/PressRoom/PressReleases/9327-26)).
- [[issuers/securitize|Securitize]] completed its acquisition of River One, a New York asset-backed warehouse lender, its first acquisition since listing in July 2026 ([Securitize](https://investors.securitize.io/news/news-details/2026/Securitize-Completes-Acquisition-of-River-One-Expanding-Into-6-Trillion-Asset-Backed-Finance-Market/default.aspx)).
