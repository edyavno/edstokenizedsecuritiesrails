- Five later letters in the SEC Innovation Exemption comment file (4-927) are now recorded on the [[concepts/innovation-exemption|Innovation Exemption]] page. Securitize (12 Nov) asks the SEC to require permissioning in the token itself rather than only in a pool allowlist, with freeze, burn and reissue as the fallback, and also to permit PropAMMs, define "permissionless," and let issuers waive the 30-day wait ([Securitize](https://www.sec.gov/comments/S7-2026-32/4927-1134705-4042498.pdf)). Chainlink Labs (11 Nov), a vendor of the services it discusses, proposes FAQs on comparable trade reports, corporate-action reconciliation and pricing data ([Chainlink Labs](https://www.sec.gov/comments/4-927/4927-1133160-4036827.pdf)). Shadow SEC Statement No. 17 (filed 10 Nov; statement dated 19 Oct), by five securities-law scholars including former SEC General Counsel John C. Coates, argues that the order is a legislative rule that skipped APA notice and comment and will fragment liquidity ([Shadow SEC Statement No. 17](https://www.sec.gov/comments/4-927/4927-1131060-4029687.pdf)). The American Securities Association (6 Nov) puts the opposing view, including offshore Covered Firm affiliates and cybersecurity as disclosure-only; hack figures in that letter are reported by TRM Labs, with ASA also citing CBS News as saying Chainalysis concurs in the attribution ([ASA](https://www.sec.gov/comments/4-927/4927-1122019-3994146.pdf)). Matter Labs (4 Nov) asks that validity (zero-knowledge) proofs verified on a public chain be able to satisfy the public, permissionless ledger condition ([Matter Labs](https://www.sec.gov/comments/4-927/4927-1121099-3989526.pdf)). The Securitize, Chainlink, Shadow SEC and Matter Labs letters are also noted on the related token-primitives, who-can-buy, venue-architecture, Securitize, corporate-actions, Chainlink, reference-architecture and regulation pages. Comment letters are advocacy, not law.
- [[issuers/ondo-global-markets|Ondo Stocks]] no longer describes Off-Hours trading only as covering "a select set of assets." A 16 Nov 2026 snapshot of Ondo's Off-Hours docs lists 32 enabled assets on Ethereum, BNB Chain and Solana, for weekend, holiday and early-close sessions, with instant mint and redeem and dynamic per-asset limits ([Ondo Off-Hours](https://docs.ondo.finance/ondo-stocks/off-hours-trading)). The same snapshot is noted on the settlement and reference-architecture pages. The list and limits can change.
- The [[concepts/token-primitives|token primitives]] page now records Base's B20 Policy Registry: policy scopes default to always-allow, one shared allowlist or blocklist can bind many tokens, and the events include seize, a freeze-style blocked burn, and pause. That design is contrasted with ERC-3643 and with Securitize's token-level permissioning ask ([Base updatePolicy](https://docs.base.org/specifications/b20/reference/interfaces/ib20/update-policy)). A short note is also on the [[issuers/coinbase-tokenized-stocks|Coinbase]] page.
- [[concepts/international|International regimes]] now includes Cambodia: on 13 Nov 2026 SBI Holdings said it had signed an MOU with the National Bank of Cambodia for a government bond tokenization proof of concept on Canton, with the central bank as registrar and small-unit retail holdings through local banks. This is an MOU for a PoC, not a live service ([SBI Holdings](https://www.sbigroup.co.jp/news/pdf/2026/1113_a.pdf)).
