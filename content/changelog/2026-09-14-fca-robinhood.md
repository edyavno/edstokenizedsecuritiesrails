- FCA and Bank of England published FS26/1 on tokenisation in wholesale markets (123 responses); a joint roadmap is due later in 2026 ([FCA](https://www.fca.org.uk/publications/feedback-statements/fs26-1-tokenisation-wholesale-markets)).
- Robinhood's CEO said in-kind redemption and voting are coming for Robinhood Stock Tokens, after AMC's CEO objected to AMC tokens ([CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/business/2026/09/14/robinhood-plans-share-redemptions-voting-rights-for-stock-tokens-after-criticism)).
- Coinbase's CEO said voting rights are coming to Coinbase tokenized stocks (CoinDesk).
