- Canton Strategic Holdings (Nasdaq: CNTN) announced the first trades of its issuer-sponsored tokenized common stock on TSV LLC, in a permissioned pool on the Tradecraft AMM, settled atomically on Canton. Five North (seller) moved its position from a broker-dealer account onto the company's onchain register; DRW Cumberland bought; Flowdesk provided liquidity. Size and price were not disclosed ([CNTN release](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/first-trades-of-tokenized-us-equity-under-sec-innovation-exemption-executed-on-the-canton-network-302929795.html)).
- Fairmint TA LLC, the company's SEC-registered transfer agent, keeps the official onchain record and the allow list of verified holders and wallets ([CNTN release](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/first-trades-of-tokenized-us-equity-under-sec-innovation-exemption-executed-on-the-canton-network-302929795.html)).
