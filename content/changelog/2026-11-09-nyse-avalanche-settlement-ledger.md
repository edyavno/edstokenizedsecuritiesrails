- NYSE and Ava Labs said Avalanche will provide the core ledger technology for the clearing firm on the NYSE-affiliated tokenized securities platform, which still needs regulatory approval ([NYSE on X](https://x.com/NYSE/status/2119812983224213903); [Ava Labs](https://www.avalanche.com/about/blog/wall-streets-next-chapter-is-being-built-on-avalanche)). Stocks may be issued on several chains, and matching stays on NYSE's existing Pillar engine (news reports: [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/news/business/2026-11-09-nyse-settle-tokenized-stocks-avalanche-planned-24-7-market-436208); [CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/business/2026/11/09/nyse-picks-avalanche-to-settle-tokenized-stocks-amid-wall-street-s-blockchain-race)). The Wall Street Journal reports a fourth-quarter launch target without stating the year.
- WSJ detail: Avalanche is the only settlement chain (issuance still multi-chain); NYSE picked it after testing several providers for speed, throughput and a permissioned network that meets compliance rules ([WSJ](https://www.wsj.com/finance/regulation/nyse-to-settle-tokenized-stocks-on-avalanche-blockchain-038ce412)).
