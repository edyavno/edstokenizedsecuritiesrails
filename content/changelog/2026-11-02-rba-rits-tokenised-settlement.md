holders: No direct effect on holders yet; this is about how banks and platforms might settle tokenised trades in central bank money in Australia.

- Australia added as a jurisdiction (no securities rule collected). The Reserve Bank of Australia's consultation (3 Sep to 30 Oct 2026) sets out ways to link token platforms to its RITS settlement system for delivery versus payment, without choosing one ([RBA](https://www.rba.gov.au/payments-and-infrastructure/tokenised-money/the-role-of-rits-in-supporting-settlement-in-a-tokenised-ecosystem/pdf/the-role-of-rits-in-supporting-settlement-in-a-tokenised-ecosystem.pdf)).
