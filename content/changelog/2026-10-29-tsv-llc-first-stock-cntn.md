- TSV LLC's notice v1.3 (29 Oct 2026) names tokenized Canton Strategic Holdings (Nasdaq: CNTN) as its first stock, with Fairmint TA LLC as transfer agent. It is issuer-sponsored, so the 30-day third-party objection window doesn't apply ([TSV LLC](https://www.tokenizedstockvenue.com/notice.html)).
- CNTN's 8-K press release says Fairmint keeps the official onchain ownership record, holders get the same rights as Nasdaq holders, and trading on Canton is expected as early as 2 Nov 2026 ([CNTN 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1861657/000149315226049196/ex99-1.htm)).
