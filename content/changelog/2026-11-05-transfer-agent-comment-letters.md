- Comment letters on the SEC's proposed transfer agent rules (File S7-2026-30) pull in different directions: Nasdaq asks for interoperable onchain and offchain master files and fungibility at the recordkeeping layer ([Nasdaq](https://www.sec.gov/comments/S7-2026-30/s7202630-1122619-3995826.pdf)); Coinbase asks that holders be identifiable by wallet address without a mandatory name and mailing address ([Coinbase](https://www.sec.gov/comments/S7-2026-30/s7202630-1119719-3983946.pdf)); Fairmint proposes per-venue co-transfer agents and a functional test of "exclusive control" ([Fairmint](https://www.sec.gov/comments/S7-2026-30/s7202630-1120639-3987647.pdf)).
- These are advocacy, not law. See [[topics/regulation|Regulation]] and [[build/trading-reference-architecture#proposed-by-fairmint-not-current-law-or-production|Proposed by Fairmint, not current law or production]].
