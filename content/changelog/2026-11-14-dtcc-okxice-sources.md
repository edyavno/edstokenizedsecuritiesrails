- DTCC's Tokenization Service FAQs are now recorded on the [[infrastructure/dtcc-tokenization-pilot|DTCC Tokenization Service]] page: the DTC Digital Omnibus Account, Participant-only conversion orders and wallet registration, inter-network burn-and-mint during conversion hours, ComposerX LedgerScan, a free-of-value cash leg, destokenization before unsupported corporate-action record dates, and claw-back or force-transfer if a token is lost or stolen ([DTCC FAQs](https://www.dtcc.com/products-and-services/digital-assets/tokenization/hub)). The FAQs still name only Canton and Hyperledger Besu as live networks and say additional networks are expected in 2027; they do not overwrite the 12 Nov 2026 plan to connect the service to Solana in the second half of 2027. A 12 Aug 2026 DTCC framework describes how it evaluates Layer 1 and Layer 2 networks and does not name specific chains ([DTCC](https://files.dtcc.com/download/assets/evaluation-framework-l1-l2-blockchain-networks.pdf/8e9c53b2965e11f19a98ca3087bcb34c)).
- DTC Important Notice B#25240-26, dated 6 Nov 2026, postponed the November 2026 DRS modernization go-live. Subject to regulatory approval, DTC expects to implement no later than 30 Jun 2027, and will announce the specific date in a later notice. The notice does not say the SEC has approved SR-DTC-2026-012 ([DTC notice](https://files.dtcc.com/download/assets/25240-26/306b2894da2f11f192c2a2d2c72ada28)). The same timeline is updated on the [[concepts/issuer-sponsored-token-coalition|Issuer Sponsored Token Coalition]] page.
- After go-live, Morgan Stanley said it is among the first banks to tokenize US equities and Treasuries through the service ([Morgan Stanley on X](https://x.com/MorganStanley/status/2117727271377125454)). The Block reported that the firm expects to support tokenized equities on its alternative trading system in the first quarter of 2027, later than its previous H2 2026 target; that ATS date is The Block's reporting ([The Block](https://www.theblock.co/news/markets/2026-11-04-morgan-stanley-among-first-banks-to-tokenize-us-equities-treasurys-through-dtccs-service-432633)). Société Générale described accepting \$10 million of tokenized US Treasuries as collateral on Canton from the service's soft launch ([Société Générale](https://wholesale.banking.societegenerale.com/fileadmin/user_upload/groupe_AMER/pdf/Societe_Generale_Completes_Tokenized_US_Treasury_Collateral_Transaction_on_Canton.pdf)). Alpaca described July trial activity tokenizing SPY and CRCL on Canton and Besu ([Alpaca](https://alpaca.markets/blog/alpaca-joins-launch-of-dtcc-tokenization-service/)). Those named uses are the companies' own claims.
- On [[platforms-venues/okxice-tsv|OKXICE TSV]], Applied Optoelectronics is recorded as the one issuer in this record to have given an on-the-record objection reason, via a spokesperson quote in Fortune; the page keeps the official 5 Nov notice count of 67 objectors and does not adopt Fortune's counts ([Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/11/05/apple-home-depot-list-objectors-tokenized-stock-plan-nyse-owner/)). ICE's 5 Mar 2026 minority investment in OKX, at a stated \$25 billion valuation with an ICE board seat, is now noted as distinct from the 50/50 OKXICE LLC joint venture and from NYSE's separate tokenized-equities platform ([ICE](https://ir.theice.com/press/news-details/2026/ICE-Makes-Investment-in-OKX-Establishing-Strategic-Relationship/)). Securitize's already-ingested 13 Nov article is cross-cited for its claim that Convertible Entitlement Tokens are the mechanism supporting the OKXICE launch ([Securitize](https://medium.com/securitize/the-tsv-stack-you-bring-execution-and-liquidity-we-bring-the-stock-b2cb64c744ed)).
