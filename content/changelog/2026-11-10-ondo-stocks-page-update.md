- The [[issuers/ondo-global-markets|Ondo Stocks]] page now leads with the official name (previously Ondo Global Markets) and a source-backed asset count from Ondo's token registry: 452 tokenized stocks and ETFs as of 10 Nov 2026, with Ondo Intelligent Portfolios recorded as a separate product ([token registry](https://docs.ondo.finance/partner-docs/tokens)).
- New sections cover eligibility and onboarding, mint and redeem mechanics, dividends and fees, and a comparison with [[issuers/xstocks-backed|xStocks]], [[issuers/robinhood-stock-tokens|Robinhood Stock Tokens]] and [[issuers/dinari|Dinari]]. A holder who redeems with Ondo receives USDon or USDC, not the underlying share; the tokens are structured notes, not title to the share ([investing and redeeming](https://docs.ondo.finance/ondo-stocks/investing-and-redeeming); [legal](https://docs.ondo.finance/ondo-stocks/legal-and-regulatory)).
