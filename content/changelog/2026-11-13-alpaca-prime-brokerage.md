- In a 15 Oct 2026 interview on NYSE Live, Alpaca's co-founder and CEO, Yoshi Yokokawa — not NYSE staff — said the firm planned to launch a prime brokerage business "next year" (the host's wording; the interview date implies 2027) to support market-maker liquidity with financing, margin, and multiple asset classes. That is a plan described in the interview, not a launch. The same interview is now noted on the [[build/trading-reference-architecture#primary-market-mint-and-redeem-broker-custodied-model|ITN mint and redeem]] section and on [[topics/custody|Custody]] for his description of Alpaca's clearing and custody role, including a clearing-broker license and DTC membership as Alpaca's own account ([NYSE Live](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=c8VAgn88uqs)).
- Yokokawa also put US equities tokenized as SPV-wrapped tokens at about \$3.5B, against an approximately \$86T US equity market cap, which he said was less than one basis point. That is his estimate as of mid-October 2026, not a verified market figure, and it sits alongside Bitquery's 31 Oct \$3.2B third-party estimate on [[topics/who-can-buy|Who can buy]].
