holders: If you were hoping to buy a token of NVIDIA, Costco or NIKE on OKXICE, those companies have now said no, so those tokens can't trade there.

- OKXICE's 30 Oct notice lists 50 objecting companies, up from 38 on 28 Oct. The 12 new ones are CrowdStrike, Corning, Micron, IREN, USA Rare Earth, Bloom Energy, NIKE, NVIDIA, Palo Alto Networks, Costco, Reddit and Hewlett Packard Enterprise ([OKXICE notice, 30 Oct](https://www.okx.com/cdn/assets/okfe/web3-activities/okxicetsv/notices/OKXICE_Tokenized_Securities_Venue_Public_Notice_26.10.30.pdf)). There is still no trading start date. See [[platforms-venues/okxice-tsv|OKXICE TSV]].
