- On 16 Nov 2026 Zelle demonstrated a confidential transfer of $1.25 million in ZelleUSD (ZLUSD) on stage at Solana Breakpoint, between demo banks labeled Bank Alpha and Bank Beta. The official @solana video of the demo is marked "Solana devnet." This is a stablecoin cash-leg example, not a tokenized deposit or a tokenized security. ZelleUSD is issued for Zelle by Anchorage Digital Bank and is already minted on Solana; Anchorage's 30 Sep 2026 reserve report records 85,151 redeemable tokens outstanding and states that the Solana contract is 7NNkMtGACBbwJdMRYD2KchTKm2xBwnsb4bT7YSfRUWZ4. Zelle's 11 June 2026 release said India would be the first country where U.S. consumers can use Zelle to send money overseas, with initial availability expected before the end of 2026, but that release does not say the corridor uses ZelleUSD, and no launch date has been given ([Zelle release](https://www.zelle.com/press-releases/zelle-heads-to-india-unveils-zelleusdsm-stablecoin-other-markets), [Anchorage ZLUSD attestations](https://www.anchorage.com/platform/zlusd-reserve-attestations), [30 Sep 2026 ZLUSD Reserve Report](https://learn.anchorage.com/09.30.26_ZLUSD-Stablecoin-Attestation-Report-signed.pdf), [@solana](https://x.com/solana/status/2122284580405236216)).
