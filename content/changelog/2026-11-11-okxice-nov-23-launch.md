- [[platforms-venues/okxice-tsv|OKXICE TSV]]: OKX announced that trading for eligible U.S. customers is due to begin on 23 November 2026, with more than 70 assets expected to be listed, including 59 Direxion ETFs, Strategy common and preferred shares, Better.com, and Coinbase, Circle, Robinhood, BitGo and Securitize (the last six are on the 4 Oct list and were not among the 67 objectors on the 5 Nov notice). Securitize is named as the tokenizer; tokens are described as security entitlements backed 1:1, held in self-custodial wallets, on Uniswap v4 pools on X Layer. Access is limited to eligible U.S. OKX Inc. customers with a CeDeFi Wallet, identity verification and OKXICE onboarding ([OKX](https://www.okx.com/en-us/learn/okxice-tsv-launch)). A spokesperson said not all assets will be available at the start ([CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/business/2026/11/10/okxice-to-debut-tokenized-stock-trading-with-strategy-coinbase-circle-this-month)). CoinDesk's 56-of-62 objector tally differs from the notice's 67; CoinDesk's "second venue after TSV LLC" claim is attributed to CoinDesk.
