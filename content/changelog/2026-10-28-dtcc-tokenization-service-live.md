- DTC launched the DTCC Tokenization Service with 20 signed DTC participants, on Canton and DTCC's private Besu network; more networks in 2027 ([DTCC](https://www.dtcc.com/press-releases/2026/dtcc-tokenization-service-goes-live)).
- Day 1 scope: 34 securities, 1:1 backed by DTC holdings, conversions 2 a.m.-6 p.m. ET business days targeting 15 minutes or less, 24/7 transfers between registered wallets ([DTCC tear sheet](https://dtcclearning.com/documents/dtcc-digital-assets/digital-asset-solutions/tokenization-service/5699-dtcc-tokenization-service-product-tear-sheet-and-high-level-roadmap/file.html)).
