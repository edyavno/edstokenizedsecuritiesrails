- The FSRA Listing Authority admitted further BTECH Holdings Limited bStocks to the Official List after the 11 June 2026 initial admission, in notices dated 12 June through 28 October 2026. The [[issuers/bstocks|bStocks]] page now lists those later admissions, with security names as printed on each official notice (raw/269-289). Later notices also admit Certificates over ETFs, ADRs and ADSs, as labelled on the notice. This is not a statement of how many bStocks are outstanding or tracked elsewhere.
