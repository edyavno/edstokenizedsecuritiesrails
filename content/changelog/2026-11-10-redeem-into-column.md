- The [[comparisons/implementations-by-model|implementations compared by model]] table now includes a "What can you redeem into?" column, sourced from issuer docs (and, where this pass did not re-open those docs, from existing vault pages).
- [[issuers/xstocks-backed|xStocks]]: after KYC, redeem with the issuer for USDC, USDG or USDT ($5,000 minimum stated for direct issuer flow) or in-kind shares into an Alpaca brokerage account via xPort ([FAQ](https://docs.xstocks.fi/docs/frequently-asked-questions); [Market Flow](https://docs.xstocks.fi/docs/issuance-and-redemption/market-flow); [xPort](https://docs.xstocks.fi/docs/issuance-and-redemption/in-kind-flow-xport)).
- [[issuers/robinhood-stock-tokens|Robinhood Stock Tokens]] (Jersey): cash only; the prospectus excludes physical delivery of the underlying ([base prospectus, 25 June 2026](https://cdn.robinhood.com/assets/robinhood/legal/rhj_base_prospectus.pdf)). EU Classic Stock Tokens are a separate product: sell back to Robinhood Europe in the app for EUR; they cannot be redeemed for shares ([KID, 1 July 2026](https://cdn.robinhood.com/assets/robinhood/legal/stock_tokens_kid_eu.pdf); [EU FAQ](https://robinhood.com/eu/en/support/articles/stock-tokens-faq/)).
- [[issuers/dinari|Dinari dShares]]: sell or burn for payment tokens (stablecoin proceeds); the custodian sells the share. No in-kind delivery found in issuer docs ([placing orders](https://docs.dinari.com/docs/placing-orders); [dShares](https://dinari.com/dshares)).
- [[issuers/ondo-global-markets|Ondo Stocks]] already recorded the same point: redeem with Ondo for USDon or USDC; notes, not title to the share.
