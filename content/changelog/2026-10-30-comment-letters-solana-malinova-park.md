- Solana Policy Institute (22 Oct 2026) backs the order and asks the SEC to define "public, permissionless distributed ledger" by who controls ordering, validation, finality and rule changes ([Solana Policy Institute](https://www.sec.gov/comments/4-927/4927-1106380-3893468.pdf)).
- Academics Katya Malinova and Andreas Park (23 Oct 2026) say the volume caps block the arbitrage that keeps token prices in line and raise trading costs; they ask for the caps to be removed or set per stock ([Malinova and Park](https://www.sec.gov/comments/4-927/4927-1107499-3908312.pdf)).
- DeFi Education Fund (16 Oct 2026) welcomes the order and asks for safe harbors for decentralized exchange developers ([DeFi Education Fund](https://www.sec.gov/comments/4-927/4927-1096319-3852226.pdf)).
- Updated: [[concepts/innovation-exemption|Innovation Exemption]], glossary (Public, permissionless ledger).
