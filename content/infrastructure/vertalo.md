*In plain English: Vertalo keeps companies' official shareholder records and links tokens to them, so token holders get dividends and votes.*

Vertalo is an SEC-registered [[concepts/glossary#transfer-agent|transfer agent]] whose platform links tokenized securities to the shareholder records that carry a holder's dividends and votes ([Bullish](https://www.bullish.com/us/news-insights/issuer-sponsored-token-coalition-gains-momentum-as-qrt-vertalo-and-zerohash-join)). It launched its platform in 2019 and is led by founder and CEO Dave Hendricks ([Bullish](https://www.bullish.com/us/news-insights/issuer-sponsored-token-coalition-gains-momentum-as-qrt-vertalo-and-zerohash-join)).

On 26 Oct 2026 it joined the [[concepts/issuer-sponsored-token-coalition|Issuer Sponsored Token Coalition]]. Hendricks said [[concepts/glossary#issuer-sponsored-token|issuer-sponsored tokens]] work because the shareholder records and the token agree, and that this agreement drives routine [[concepts/glossary#corporate-action|corporate actions]] ([Bullish](https://www.bullish.com/us/news-insights/issuer-sponsored-token-coalition-gains-momentum-as-qrt-vertalo-and-zerohash-join)).
