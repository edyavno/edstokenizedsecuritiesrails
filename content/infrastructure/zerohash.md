*In plain English: Zerohash sells brokerages the plumbing to offer tokenized assets to their customers.*

Zerohash markets a Tokenization Engine to brokerages ([Zerohash](https://zerohash.com/industries/brokerages)).

Bullish describes zerohash as regulated onchain infrastructure powering Morgan Stanley, Interactive Brokers, Public, tastytrade, OnePay and Visa. It joined the [[concepts/issuer-sponsored-token-coalition|Issuer Sponsored Token Coalition]] on 26 Oct 2026 and says it plans to extend its stack, with 10 million onboarded customers, to meet the coalition's token standards ([Bullish](https://www.bullish.com/us/news-insights/issuer-sponsored-token-coalition-gains-momentum-as-qrt-vertalo-and-zerohash-join)).
