*In plain English: tZERO builds the record-keeping and trading plumbing for tokenized shares, and is lined up to be one of NYSE's approved token record-keepers.*

On 31 Aug 2026 tZERO and ICE signed an MOU making tZERO a design partner for the [[concepts/glossary#transfer-agent|transfer agent]] and broker-dealer infrastructure of NYSE's planned tokenized securities platform. Once regulatory, technology and operational requirements are met, tZERO is expected to be designated an approved digital transfer agent and subscriber ([tZERO](https://www.tzero.com/news/tzero-and-ice-agree-to-collaborate-on-infrastructure-for-public-tokenized-securities-markets)).

ICE is also investing in tZERO's latest funding round (amount not disclosed) and gets a licence to tZERO's blockchain patent portfolio, 23 patent families and 103 patents covering compliance-aware transfers, upgradeable smart contracts and corporate-action handling. The two will look at using tZERO tokenized assets as collateral at ICE's clearing houses ([tZERO](https://www.tzero.com/news/tzero-and-ice-agree-to-collaborate-on-infrastructure-for-public-tokenized-securities-markets)).

It is one of six NYSE transfer agent partners as of 2 Nov 2026 ([[platforms-venues/nyse-ice-tokenized-platform#transfer-agent-partners|NYSE / ICE]]; [Crypto Briefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/nyse-expands-tokenization-push-with-firms-serving-nearly-99-of-sp-500/)).
