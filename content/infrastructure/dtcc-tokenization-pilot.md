*In plain English: DTC, the US central securities depository, launched the DTCC Tokenization Service (DTS) on 28 Oct 2026 under an 11 Dec 2025 SEC no-action letter. It turns eligible DTC-held securities into tokenized entitlements on Canton and DTCC's private Besu network.*

DTC operates a tokenization pilot under a 11 Dec 2025 SEC no-action letter ([SEC](https://www.sec.gov/files/rules/sro/nasdaq/2026/34-105047.pdf)); 24X Exchange filed rules referencing it ([SEC](https://www.sec.gov/rules-regulations/self-regulatory-organization-rulemaking/sr-24x-2026-20)).

## What is the DTCC Tokenization Service?
The DTCC Tokenization Service (DTS) is a DTC service, live since 28 Oct 2026, that converts eligible DTC-held securities into tokenized security entitlements on the Canton Network and DTCC's private Besu network, and back again ([DTCC](https://www.dtcc.com/press-releases/2026/dtcc-tokenization-service-goes-live)). Only DTC Participants use it directly (20 had signed agreements at launch), and it operates under an SEC staff no-action letter dated 11 Dec 2025 ([DTCC](https://www.dtcc.com/press-releases/2026/dtcc-tokenization-service-goes-live); [SEC NAL](https://www.sec.gov/files/tm/no-action/dtc-nal-121125.pdf)).

## DTC no-action letter (11 Dec 2025)
Staff relief lets DTC participants record security entitlements on [[concepts/glossary#dlt|DLT]] ("Tokenized Entitlements") for Russell 1000 stocks, major-index [[concepts/glossary#etf|ETFs]] and Treasuries; tokens have no collateral or settlement value for Net Debit Cap / Collateral Monitor ([SEC NAL](https://www.sec.gov/files/tm/no-action/dtc-nal-121125.pdf); [Mayer Brown](https://www.mayerbrown.com/-/media/files/perspectives-events/publications/2025/12/sec-staff-no-action-relief-for-dtc-pilot-provides-a-pathway-for-tokenized-securities.pdf)). The letter is withdrawn three years from the date DTC launches the service ([SEC NAL](https://www.sec.gov/files/tm/no-action/dtc-nal-121125.pdf)).
## Live (28 Oct 2026)
DTC launched the DTCC Tokenization Service (DTS) on 28 Oct 2026 ([DTCC](https://www.dtcc.com/press-releases/2026/dtcc-tokenization-service-goes-live)). Tokenized security entitlements convert between traditional and tokenized form and go to users' wallets of choice, keeping the ownership rights, investor protections and economic entitlements of the traditional securities ([DTCC](https://www.dtcc.com/press-releases/2026/dtcc-tokenization-service-goes-live)).

- **Participants:** 20 DTC participants have signed agreements: Alpaca, Apex Clearing Corporation, Barclays, Bank of America, BNP Paribas, Brown Brothers Harriman, Citadel Securities, Citi, Clear Street, DriveWealth, Edward Jones, Fidelity Investments, Interchange Clearing, J.P. Morgan, Marex, Mirae Asset Securities (USA) Inc., Morgan Stanley, State Street, UBS and Velocity Capital LLC. More will be onboarded on a rolling basis ([DTCC](https://www.dtcc.com/press-releases/2026/dtcc-tokenization-service-goes-live)).
  - Participant Interchange Clearing runs execution, clearing and custody on one real-time platform with direct DTC and NSCC links ([Interchange](https://www.interchange.com/blog/interchange-clearing-to-access-the-dtcc-tokenization-service)).
  - After go-live, Morgan Stanley said it is among the first banks to tokenize "U.S. Equity and U.S. Treasuries" through DTS ([Morgan Stanley on X](https://x.com/MorganStanley/status/2117727271377125454), 3 Nov 2026). A quote card on that post, from Amy Oldenburg, Head of Digital Assets Strategy, says the experience "will inform the continued development of the firm's digital asset capabilities as the market evolves." The Block reported that Morgan Stanley expects to support tokenized equities on its alternative trading system in the first quarter of 2027, later than its previous H2 2026 target, and that Oldenburg had told The Block the previous month the bank was building the necessary infrastructure ([The Block](https://www.theblock.co/news/markets/2026-11-04-morgan-stanley-among-first-banks-to-tokenize-us-equities-treasurys-through-dtccs-service-432633), 4 Nov 2026). The ATS date is The Block's reporting, not the X post.
  - Société Générale said its Prime Services & Clearing division accepted USD 10 million of tokenized US Treasuries as collateral on Canton, "using assets tokenized as part of the soft launch" of DTCC's Tokenization Service, and that it intends to develop recurring collateral movements and, over time, explore agency and principal repo, subject to internal approvals and regulatory requirements ([Société Générale](https://wholesale.banking.societegenerale.com/fileadmin/user_upload/groupe_AMER/pdf/Societe_Generale_Completes_Tokenized_US_Treasury_Collateral_Transaction_on_Canton.pdf), 2 Nov 2026).
  - Alpaca said it joined DTCC's Industry Working Group in May 2026 and that, in the July 2026 production event, it helped partners tokenize DTC-held SPY and CRCL holdings on the Canton Network and DTCC's private Besu network, then redeem them through Alpaca's Instant Tokenization Network ([Alpaca](https://alpaca.markets/blog/alpaca-joins-launch-of-dtcc-tokenization-service/), 29 Oct 2026). Those ticker names and the July activity are Alpaca's account.
- **Networks:** Canton Network and DTCC's private Besu network today, with more networks in 2027 ([DTCC](https://www.dtcc.com/press-releases/2026/dtcc-tokenization-service-goes-live)). On 12 Nov 2026 DTCC said it plans to connect the Tokenization Service to Solana's L1, with DTC-tokenized assets expected to be available on Solana in the second half of 2027. The integration is expected to support rapid conversion of eligible DTC-custodied assets into tokenized form and related token lifecycle functionality. DTCC says tokens on Solana, and on all blockchain networks integrated with the service, will retain the same investor protections, ownership rights and regulatory safeguards that apply to traditionally held securities ([DTCC](https://www.dtcc.com/press-releases/2026/dtcc-tokenization-service-to-connect-with-solana)). DTCC's product FAQs, retrieved 14 Nov 2026, still name only the Canton Network and Hyperledger Besu as networks Participants can register wallets on, and say DTC "expects to add additional networks in 2027"; that FAQ does not name Solana, Stellar or Arc ([DTCC FAQs](https://www.dtcc.com/products-and-services/digital-assets/tokenization/hub)). A 12 Aug 2026 DTCC framework describes the risk-based, SIFMU-aligned process DTC uses to evaluate Layer 1 and Layer 2 networks for its digital asset services, including early risk screening, client demand and operational fitness; the framework itself does not name any specific chain ([DTCC](https://files.dtcc.com/download/assets/evaluation-framework-l1-l2-blockchain-networks.pdf/8e9c53b2965e11f19a98ca3087bcb34c)).
- **Ecosystem:** collaborators and partners include BitGo Bank & Trust, Blockdaemon, Broadridge, DFNS, DRW, Fireblocks, HIFI, Kaleido, Matter Labs, [[issuers/ondo-global-markets|Ondo]], Prometheum Capital, Talos, Taurus, Temple Digital Group, Tradeweb, Transcend and Virtu ([DTCC](https://www.dtcc.com/press-releases/2026/dtcc-tokenization-service-goes-live)).
- **Design input:** more than 150 firms in the DTC Digital Assets Solutions Industry Working Group ([DTCC](https://www.dtcc.com/press-releases/2026/dtcc-tokenization-service-goes-live)).
- **Scale:** DTC has more than \$114T in assets under custody ([DTCC](https://www.dtcc.com/press-releases/2026/dtcc-tokenization-service-goes-live)).

Scope at launch follows the Day 1 tear sheet below ([DTCC tear sheet](https://dtcclearning.com/documents/dtcc-digital-assets/digital-asset-solutions/tokenization-service/5699-dtcc-tokenization-service-product-tear-sheet-and-high-level-roadmap/file.html)).

## Progress
DTCC said the service was designed with feedback from more than 50 firms, with limited production trades planned for July 2026 and launch in October 2026 ([DTCC](https://www.dtcc.com/press-releases/2026/dtcc-advances-development-of-new-tokenization-service)). About 40 firms took part in live production trades; the 28 Oct go-live release says "more than 30 firms" completed the initial limited-production trades ([DTCC](https://www.dtcc.com/digital-assets/tokenization/live-production-trades); [DTCC press](https://www.dtcc.com/news/2026/july/15/dtcc-turns-tokenization-into-reality)).

On 23 Oct 2026 DTCC posted a Day 1 product tear sheet and an online user guide to its Learning Center ([DTCC tear sheet](https://dtcclearning.com/documents/dtcc-digital-assets/digital-asset-solutions/tokenization-service/5699-dtcc-tokenization-service-product-tear-sheet-and-high-level-roadmap/file.html)).

On 17 Nov 2026 Talos, Marex and [[infrastructure/broadridge|Broadridge]] announced the first named trading and financing workflow built on the service: Talos RFQ execution, Marex prime brokerage and Broadridge's DLX settlement orchestrator, starting with financing tokenized U.S. Treasuries beyond market hours. The three firms took part in DTCC's July 2026 production transactions; no go-live date was given ([Broadridge](https://www.broadridge.com/press-release/2026/broadridge-to-enable-institutional-trading-of-dtcc-tokenized-securities)). Separately, Alpaca's co-founder said Alpaca clients can now use its API to mint DTCC tokens from eligible securities they already hold (his claim, as reported by [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/news/business/2026-11-17-alpaca-says-tokenization-network-tops-10-billion-minting-redemption-volume-442667)).

Before launch, no go-live date was public; The Block (8 Oct 2026) reported launch was expected that month ([The Block](https://www.theblock.co/newsletters/tokenization-briefing/2026-10-08-dtcc-brings-tokenization-us-markets-417839), secondary).

## Scope at launch (Oct 2026)
DTCC's Day 1 tear sheet (23 Oct 2026) says **34** securities are available to tokenize at launch ([DTCC tear sheet](https://dtcclearning.com/documents/dtcc-digital-assets/digital-asset-solutions/tokenization-service/5699-dtcc-tokenization-service-product-tear-sheet-and-high-level-roadmap/file.html)); its earlier at-a-glance sheet said 25 ([DTCC](https://communications.dtcc.com/rs/669-QIL-921/images/DTCC%20Tokenization%20Service%20At-a-Glance.pdf)), and DTCC does not explain the change.

Other launch terms in the at-a-glance sheet, unchanged in the tear sheet:  about \$30T eligible, backed 1:1 by securities held at DTC, same [[concepts/glossary#cusip|CUSIP]] and the same rights and investor protections as book-entry securities, and 24/7 peer-to-peer transfers between DTC-registered [[concepts/glossary#wallet|wallets]] on the same chain ([DTCC](https://communications.dtcc.com/rs/669-QIL-921/images/DTCC%20Tokenization%20Service%20At-a-Glance.pdf)).

Live at launch: Canton Network (CIP-56) and Besu, DTCC's private network ([[concepts/glossary#erc-20|ERC-20]]); Stellar and Circle's Arc are anticipated in 2027 ([DTCC](https://communications.dtcc.com/rs/669-QIL-921/images/DTCC%20Tokenization%20Service%20At-a-Glance.pdf)). A 12 Nov 2026 DTCC release adds Solana, planned for the second half of 2027 ([DTCC](https://www.dtcc.com/press-releases/2026/dtcc-tokenization-service-to-connect-with-solana)). Eligible assets start with select large-cap Russell 1000 equities, select ETFs and a select set of Treasury bills, notes and bonds ([DTCC](https://communications.dtcc.com/rs/669-QIL-921/images/DTCC%20Tokenization%20Service%20At-a-Glance.pdf)).

Conversion orders run Monday to Friday, 2 a.m.-6 p.m. ET ([DTC notice DAS-001](https://files.dtcc.com/download/assets/DAS-001/8a31d732d2d311f1a30ef632b7f70f6d)), targeting 15 minutes or less; tokens carry transfer restrictions; official books and records stay with DTC; Treasury distributions and maturities pay in fiat to participants' traditional accounts ([DTCC](https://communications.dtcc.com/rs/669-QIL-921/images/DTCC%20Tokenization%20Service%20At-a-Glance.pdf)).

Roadmap for 2027 and beyond (indicative): equity/ETF corporate actions, a consolidated stock record, on-chain [[concepts/glossary#netting|netting]] and digital cash ([DTCC](https://communications.dtcc.com/rs/669-QIL-921/images/DTCC%20Tokenization%20Service%20At-a-Glance.pdf)). Coverage: [Crypto Briefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/dtcc-tokenization-service-launch-october/) (news).

Firm counts differ by stage, and all of them come from DTCC: more than 50 firms gave design feedback; its July live-trades page says about 40 firms took part; its 28 Oct go-live release says "more than 30 firms" completed the initial limited-production trades. DTCC doesn't explain the gap, so both figures are shown with their source.

## How the service works (DTCC FAQs)
DTCC's Tokenization Service FAQs, retrieved 14 Nov 2026, add operational detail that is not in the go-live release or the Day 1 tear sheet ([DTCC FAQs](https://www.dtcc.com/products-and-services/digital-assets/tokenization/hub)):

- When a Participant submits a conversion order, DTC moves the traditional securities entitlements into the **DTC Digital Omnibus Account**, the account on DTC's traditional books and records that holds, in aggregate, the underlying entitlements represented by DTC-issued tokens, and mints a corresponding token into the Participant's DTC-registered wallet. ComposerX LedgerScan, a DTCC Digital Assets product, tracks token movements across approved networks; LedgerScan combined with DTC's traditional books and records is described as providing a complete picture of a Participant's traditional and tokenized positions.
- Only DTC Participants may submit conversion orders, and only Participants may register and control wallets through DTS. DTC does not provide digital wallets; Participants may use a third-party provider or a self-custodied wallet, which must be registered before access. Clients of Participants cannot submit instructions to DTC or independently register or control wallets. DTC members for which DTC performs tax reporting or withholding are currently ineligible.
- Conversion orders run Monday to Friday, 2:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. ET, excluding holidays, and cannot be submitted outside those hours, consistent with the earlier DAS-001 window ([DTC notice DAS-001](https://files.dtcc.com/download/assets/DAS-001/8a31d732d2d311f1a30ef632b7f70f6d); [DTCC FAQs](https://www.dtcc.com/products-and-services/digital-assets/tokenization/hub)).
- Token transfers are 24/7 between DTC-registered wallets on the same supported network. During conversion hours, a token may move to a registered wallet on another approved network by being burned on the source network and minted on the destination network; the FAQs say this is done "without relying on unsupported bridge protocols."
- DTS is a free-of-value service: DTC does not process the cash side of a transaction, and Participants may use the on-chain cash solution of their choice. Tokenized securities cannot be used directly in traditional end-of-day settlement at DTC, or in NSCC or FICC clearing, until they are converted back to book-entry form.
- On-chain coupon payments "are not currently available and remain in development"; coupon payments continue in traditional currency to the Participant's traditional DTC account. Where a corporate action is not yet supported digitally, the tokenized position is converted back to book-entry form before the record date, and DTC will not automatically create a new token afterwards; the Participant must submit a new conversion request.
- DTC "maintains administrative control over DTC-issued tokens, including the ability to claw back or force transfer tokens if they are lost or stolen."
- The FAQs say DTC currently supports a select subset of eligible securities based on Participant demand, with a goal of expanding over time. The July 15, 2026 production event, in DTCC's description, included collateral pledge, securities lending, U.S. Treasury repo and delivery-versus-payment (DVP), equity DVP, equity token transfers, equity delivery-versus-delivery (DVD), and central counterparty (CCP) margin through the DTCC Collateral AppChain, which the FAQs describe as a separate DTCC product.

## DRS modernization (SR-DTC-2026-012)
DTC filed on 29 Sep 2026 to modernize the [[concepts/glossary#direct-registration-system-drs|Direct Registration System (DRS)]], which moves shares between [[concepts/glossary#cede--co|Cede & Co.]] and the holder's own name on the issuer's books; the SEC published notice on 5 Oct 2026 (Release 34-106552) ([SEC notice](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/10/05/2026-20301/self-regulatory-organizations-the-depository-trust-company-notice-of-filing-of-proposed-rule-change)). The aim is to cut processing "from days to minutes" by retiring legacy terminal and batch-file methods, offering DRS through DTC's SPA screens plus API and MQ messaging, automating the Deliver Order after a transfer agent approves a deposit, and adding status tracking and an audit trail ([SEC notice](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/10/05/2026-20301/self-regulatory-organizations-the-depository-trust-company-notice-of-filing-of-proposed-rule-change)). Only a DTC participant can start a withdrawal ([SEC notice](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/10/05/2026-20301/self-regulatory-organizations-the-depository-trust-company-notice-of-filing-of-proposed-rule-change)). The SEC notice gave an expected implementation date of 13 Nov 2026, and no later than January 2027 ([SEC notice](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/10/05/2026-20301/self-regulatory-organizations-the-depository-trust-company-notice-of-filing-of-proposed-rule-change)). On 6 Nov 2026, DTC Important Notice B#25240-26 postponed that November 2026 implementation. Subject to regulatory approval, DTC expects to implement the changes no later than 30 Jun 2027, and will announce the specific date in a later Important Notice. Until then, Participants must keep using existing legacy PTS/PBS and batch-file channels; API and MQ testing continues. The notice does not say the SEC has approved SR-DTC-2026-012 ([DTC notice B#25240-26](https://files.dtcc.com/download/assets/25240-26/306b2894da2f11f192c2a2d2c72ada28)).

Securitize's 25 Oct comment letter notes the filing does not mention DTC's tokenization service, and asks for a one-step burn-and-withdraw instruction, published processing times, [[concepts/glossary#dwac|DWAC]] modernization, a broker timing expectation, and a fast return to [[concepts/glossary#street-name|street name]] ([[issuers/securitize|Securitize]]; [Securitize letter](https://www.sec.gov/comments/SR-DTC-2026-012/srdtc2026012-1108719-3924407.pdf)).

## Watch
- Go-live: done, 28 Oct 2026 ([DTCC](https://www.dtcc.com/press-releases/2026/dtcc-tokenization-service-goes-live)). Next: new participants, added securities beyond the launch 34, and 2027 networks (Stellar, Arc, and Solana in the second half of 2027).
- DRS modernization: the November 2026 implementation was postponed. Watch for the later Important Notice with the exact date (expected no later than 30 Jun 2027, subject to regulatory approval), whether the SEC approves SR-DTC-2026-012, and whether DTC responds to the Securitize letter ([DTC notice B#25240-26](https://files.dtcc.com/download/assets/25240-26/306b2894da2f11f192c2a2d2c72ada28)).
- First named users: 20 participants named 28 Oct 2026 (see Live). After go-live, Morgan Stanley said it had tokenized US equities and Treasuries through DTS (company claim, [Morgan Stanley on X](https://x.com/MorganStanley/status/2117727271377125454)); Société Générale described a \$10 million tokenized-Treasury collateral use on Canton from the soft launch ([Société Générale](https://wholesale.banking.societegenerale.com/fileadmin/user_upload/groupe_AMER/pdf/Societe_Generale_Completes_Tokenized_US_Treasury_Collateral_Transaction_on_Canton.pdf)); Alpaca described July trial activity in SPY and CRCL (company claim, [Alpaca](https://alpaca.markets/blog/alpaca-joins-launch-of-dtcc-tokenization-service/)). Watch for which participants tokenize client positions on an ongoing basis.
- Kimber Digital Securities LLC, a Plume affiliate, said on 10 Nov 2026 that it is now an SEC-registered broker-dealer and a FINRA member, that Plume has been a member of the DTCC Digital Assets Solutions Industry Working Group since August, and that it plans tokens which would record a customer's security entitlement against the firm, with a matching entitlement at DTC. The release states that access to DTC tokenized entitlements is under development and may not yet be available, and it does not give a launch date ([PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/kimber-digital-securities-becomes-a-registered-broker-dealer-bringing-real-us-securities-onchain-302935166.html)).
