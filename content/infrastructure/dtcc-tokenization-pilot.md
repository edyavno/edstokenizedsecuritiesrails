DTC operates a tokenization pilot under a 11 Dec 2025 SEC no-action letter ([SEC](https://www.sec.gov/files/rules/sro/nasdaq/2026/34-105047.pdf)); 24X Exchange filed rules referencing it ([SEC](https://www.sec.gov/rules-regulations/self-regulatory-organization-rulemaking/sr-24x-2026-20)).

## DTC no-action letter (11 Dec 2025)
Staff relief lets DTC participants record security entitlements on DLT ("Tokenized Entitlements") for Russell 1000 stocks, major-index ETFs and Treasuries; tokens have no collateral or settlement value for Net Debit Cap / Collateral Monitor ([SEC NAL](https://www.sec.gov/files/tm/no-action/dtc-nal-121125.pdf); [Mayer Brown](https://www.mayerbrown.com/-/media/files/perspectives-events/publications/2025/12/sec-staff-no-action-relief-for-dtc-pilot-provides-a-pathway-for-tokenized-securities.pdf)). The letter is withdrawn three years from the date DTC launches the service ([SEC NAL](https://www.sec.gov/files/tm/no-action/dtc-nal-121125.pdf)).
## Progress
DTCC said the service was designed with feedback from more than 50 firms, with limited production trades planned for July 2026 and launch in October 2026 ([DTCC](https://www.dtcc.com/press-releases/2026/dtcc-advances-development-of-new-tokenization-service)). About 40 firms took part in live production trades ([DTCC](https://www.dtcc.com/digital-assets/tokenization/live-production-trades); [DTCC press](https://www.dtcc.com/news/2026/july/15/dtcc-turns-tokenization-into-reality)). On 23 Oct 2026 DTCC posted a Day 1 product tear sheet and an online user guide to its Learning Center ([DTCC tear sheet](https://dtcclearning.com/documents/dtcc-digital-assets/digital-asset-solutions/tokenization-service/5699-dtcc-tokenization-service-product-tear-sheet-and-high-level-roadmap/file.html)). As of 25 Oct 2026, no DTCC go-live press release was found; The Block (8 Oct 2026) reported launch was expected that month ([The Block](https://www.theblock.co/newsletters/tokenization-briefing/2026-10-08-dtcc-brings-tokenization-us-markets-417839), secondary).

## Scope at launch (Oct 2026)
DTCC's Day 1 tear sheet (23 Oct 2026) says **34** securities are available to tokenize at launch ([DTCC tear sheet](https://dtcclearning.com/documents/dtcc-digital-assets/digital-asset-solutions/tokenization-service/5699-dtcc-tokenization-service-product-tear-sheet-and-high-level-roadmap/file.html)); its earlier at-a-glance sheet said 25 ([DTCC](https://communications.dtcc.com/rs/669-QIL-921/images/DTCC%20Tokenization%20Service%20At-a-Glance.pdf)), and DTCC does not explain the change. Other launch terms in the at-a-glance sheet, unchanged in the tear sheet:  about \$30T eligible, backed 1:1 by securities held at DTC, same CUSIP and the same rights and investor protections as book-entry securities, and 24/7 peer-to-peer transfers between DTC-registered wallets on the same chain ([DTCC](https://communications.dtcc.com/rs/669-QIL-921/images/DTCC%20Tokenization%20Service%20At-a-Glance.pdf)). Live at launch: Canton Network (CIP-56) and Besu, DTCC's private network (ERC-20); Stellar and Circle's Arc are anticipated in 2027 ([DTCC](https://communications.dtcc.com/rs/669-QIL-921/images/DTCC%20Tokenization%20Service%20At-a-Glance.pdf)). Eligible assets start with select large-cap Russell 1000 equities, select ETFs and a select set of Treasury bills, notes and bonds ([DTCC](https://communications.dtcc.com/rs/669-QIL-921/images/DTCC%20Tokenization%20Service%20At-a-Glance.pdf)). Conversion orders run 2 a.m.-6 p.m. ET on business days, targeting 15 minutes or less; tokens carry transfer restrictions; official books and records stay with DTC; Treasury distributions and maturities pay in fiat to participants' traditional accounts ([DTCC](https://communications.dtcc.com/rs/669-QIL-921/images/DTCC%20Tokenization%20Service%20At-a-Glance.pdf)). Roadmap for 2027 and beyond (indicative): equity/ETF corporate actions, a consolidated stock record, on-chain netting and digital cash ([DTCC](https://communications.dtcc.com/rs/669-QIL-921/images/DTCC%20Tokenization%20Service%20At-a-Glance.pdf)). Coverage: [Crypto Briefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/dtcc-tokenization-service-launch-october/) (news). Firm counts differ by source and stage: more than 50 firms gave design feedback and about 40 took part in live production trades (DTCC); Crypto Briefing's "more than 30" is secondary and is not used here.
