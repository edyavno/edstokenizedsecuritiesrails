*In plain English: BlackRock's tokenized money market fund, BUIDL, is an early example that later stock-token projects copied.*

BlackRock's BUIDL fund (launched 20 Mar 2024) used Securitize as [[concepts/glossary#transfer-agent|transfer agent]], tokenization platform and placement agent ([Nasdaq PR](https://www.nasdaq.com/press-release/blackrock-launches-its-first-tokenized-fund-buidl-on-the-ethereum-network-2024-03-20)). Adjacent to equities: a fund, not a stock. See also [[issuers/wisdomtree|WisdomTree]] (funds).

On 12 Nov 2026 Securitize and Ethena Labs announced a 24/7 liquidity facility for BUIDL, supported by Ethena and operated through Securitize. It lets eligible investors exchange BUIDL for supported stablecoins, including USDC, and convert those stablecoins back into BUIDL, outside traditional market hours. The facility launches with \$50 million of initial liquidity (\$25 million USDC and \$25 million BUIDL). The release says up to another \$50 million may be made available within about an hour, and that references to a "\$100 million facility" describe maximum capacity, not an amount continuously funded or immediately available ([Securitize](https://investors.securitize.io/news/news-details/2026/Securitize-and-Ethena-Launch-100-Million-Liquidity-Facility-for-BlackRocks-BUIDL/default.aspx)).

A later bank example is HSBC's 19 Nov 2026 launch of Class SD, a tokenised unit class of a Hong Kong dollar money market fund that eligible customers can buy with HSBC RedCoin from a minimum of HKD 100; see [[concepts/international|International regimes]] ([HSBC](https://www.about.hsbc.com.hk/news-and-media/hsbc-redcoin-expands-into-wealth-with-tokenised-fund-access)).
