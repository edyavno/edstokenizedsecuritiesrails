## Why does the same stock have several tokens?

The same listed stock can have more than one token, issued by different firms, with different legal rights. Display tickers often use a suffix, but that is an issuer convention, not a single standard, and onchain metadata can change. Identify a token by its contract address, not by the ticker alone. See [[concepts/glossary#ticker-suffix|ticker suffix]] ([Base docs](https://docs.base.org/build-on-base/integrate-defi/list-tokenized-stocks); [Backpack listings](https://learn.backpack.exchange/blog/tokenized-nvidia-nvda)). Examples below use widely cited large-cap names already on this wiki. This is not a complete token list, and a suffix is not documented here unless an official page or official API retrieved on 12 Nov 2026 showed it.

| Pattern | Example | Issuer | Typical chains | What you hold |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Suffix `x` | NVDAx, TSLAx | [[issuers/xstocks-backed\|xStocks]] (Backed) | Solana, Ethereum, TON, Ink | A tracker certificate: economic exposure, not shareholder rights ([Kraken FAQ](https://support.kraken.com/articles/xstocks-faq); [xStocks FAQ](https://docs.xstocks.fi/docs/frequently-asked-questions)). The `x` ending is used throughout Kraken's FAQ; the official issuer FAQ does not itself define a suffix rule. |

## What are the four ways a stock token can be built?

In one reader-friendly lens, a stock token is wrapped, pooled, mirrored or natively issued.

The table above is grouped by the [[concepts/legal-taxonomy|SEC staff taxonomy]]. [Simon Taylor](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/sytaylor_there-are-4-models-of-stock-tokens-each-share-7526006201432686592-ntwC) has described a reader-friendly lens with four models, each trading off shareholder rights, innovation and backward compatibility (LinkedIn post, retrieved 10 Nov 2026; short link: [lnkd.in/p/duAUZtYs](https://lnkd.in/p/duAUZtYs)). The mapping here is to rows this page already tracks. It is his framing, not a legal classification or this wiki's opinion.

**Wrapped.** His example is [[issuers/xstocks-backed|xStocks]]: a custodian holds the real share, the issuer (Backed) issues a token, and the holder does not get shareholder rights. Trade-off, in his words: this maximizes innovation and can work with any stock, at the cost of those rights. On this page, [[issuers/backpack|Backpack]]-style products sit nearby in that lens, though the table's legal grouping treats them as a custodial entitlement plus a separate token.

**Pooled.** His example is [[platforms-venues/okxice-tsv|OKXICE]]: a venue tokenizes real shares, and approved traders trade against a liquidity pool. Trade-off, in his telling: the design aims to be innovative, backward compatible and to preserve shareholder rights; under the Innovation Exemption, issuers can object to their stock being traded that way.

**Mirrored.** His example is [[infrastructure/dtcc-tokenization-pilot|DTC Tokenized Entitlements]]: the original stock is immobilized, and the depository issues a token in its place. Trade-off, in his words: this maximizes backward compatibility and preserves shareholder rights, and trades off a lot of innovation.

**Natively issued.** His examples include [[issuers/superstate-opening-bell|Superstate]] (and Figure). On this table, [[infrastructure/fairmint|CNTN via Fairmint]] and [[issuers/securitize|Securitize issuer-sponsored shares]] are the other issuer-sponsored rows, where the token is the share on the company's own register. Trade-off, in his words: this maximizes innovation and rights, and gives up backward compatibility.

The wrapped and mirrored rows also differ in the claim a holder has. In a wrapped token such as [[issuers/xstocks-backed|xStocks]], a third-party issuer, Backed Assets (JE) Limited, issues a tracker certificate: the holder has that certificate, not the share itself, and it does not convey shareholder rights; the official issuer FAQ says the underlying stock is held by regulated custodians, without naming the firm ([xStocks FAQ](https://docs.xstocks.fi/docs/frequently-asked-questions); [xStocks multiplier docs](https://docs.xstocks.fi/developers/multipliers)). Kraken's FAQ, last updated 11 Nov 2026, names Alpaca Securities LLC as the custodian of the underlying shares and InCore Bank as a secondary custodian; Payward's 11 Nov 2026 release says GTN handles global execution and custody of the traditional assets that underpin each token. Those are separate company statements, recorded side by side on the [[issuers/xstocks-backed|xStocks]] page ([Kraken FAQ](https://support.kraken.com/articles/xstocks-faq); [Payward](https://www.payward.com/press-release/xstocks-uk-eu-equities)). In [[infrastructure/dtcc-tokenization-pilot|DTC Tokenized Entitlements]], securities are held at DTC and official books and records remain with DTC; on a participant's instruction, DTC mints and delivers a token representing that participant's security entitlement to the held position ([DTCC At-a-Glance](https://communications.dtcc.com/rs/669-QIL-921/images/DTCC%20Tokenization%20Service%20At-a-Glance.pdf); [SEC no-action letter](https://www.sec.gov/files/tm/no-action/dtc-nal-121125.pdf)). Under the [[concepts/legal-taxonomy|SEC staff taxonomy]], that is a custodial security entitlement (for example, under UCC Article 8), so the token evidences an interest in the position at DTC rather than a separate instrument of a third-party issuer; the staff has also noted that holders of third-party-sponsored tokens may be exposed to risks with respect to that third party, such as bankruptcy, that a holder of the underlying security would not necessarily face ([SEC staff statement, 28 Jan 2026](https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/speeches-statements/corp-fin-statement-tokenized-securities-012826-statement-tokenized-securities)).

**Permissioned at the edges, open in the middle (announced design).** Exposure on Solana, announced 16 Nov 2026 by [[issuers/superstate-opening-bell|Superstate]], Formation and Corda, gates only minting and burning to authorized non-US persons; once minted, the tokens move permissionlessly in Solana DeFi. That differs from the issuer-sponsored rows above, where holders themselves must be approved. Its disclaimer says holders have no claim on the backing asset, the issuer or Superstate. No Exposure Token was live as of the announcement ([Superstate](https://superstate.com/newsroom/superstate-formation-and-corda-join-forces-to-launch-exposure-the-official-solana-rwa-launchpad); [Exposure](https://exposuretokens.com/)).

For a five-product view by legal claim, eligibility and redemption, see [[comparisons/tokenized-stocks-compared|Tokenized stocks compared]].

Bitget says rANT "is not issued, offered, or managed by Anthropic" and "has not been approved, sponsored, or endorsed by Anthropic" ([Bitget](https://www.bitget.com/support/articles/12560603899381)).

**What Anthropic says.** The 424B4 does not mention tokenized shares: a word search of the full text found no occurrence of tokenized shares, blockchain, onchain or stablecoin ([Anthropic 424B4](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2130357/000162828026074944/anthropicpbc-424b4.htm)). Anthropic's Claude Help Center page on unauthorized stock sales, first published 11 Feb 2026 and written about private-stock transfers, says any third party selling Anthropic shares to the general public through "direct sales, 'forward contracts,' tokenized securities, or other mechanisms" is "likely offering an investment that may have no value due to our transfer restrictions" ([Claude Help Center](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/13704655-unauthorized-anthropic-stock-sales-and-investment-scams)). The page does not say how that applies after the IPO. A second page on the IPO's retail offerings in Australia, Canada, parts of the EEA, Japan, Switzerland and the UK says Anthropic "has not authorized any other firm or platform to offer, or to advertise access to, shares in the proposed retail offerings" and lists the authorized brokers; no crypto exchange or tokenizer is on that list ([Claude Help Center](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/17889756-anthropic-ipo-international-retail-brokers-and-selling-agents)). That page concerns shares in those offerings, not tokenized products, and does not mention them.

**Marketing names.** "IPO Access" (Kraken), the "IPO xStocks Campaign" (Binance Wallet) and "IPO Prime" (Bitget) are the venues' own names for their products. Kraken describes unallocated funds being refunded and allocation "decided by the IPO underwriters"; no source retrieved says what share allocation, if any, those venues or Backed received. The table records what each issuer or venue says; the tokens' legal claims are separate from the shares sold in the IPO.

## What happened on Anthropic's first trading day? (19 Nov 2026)

**On Nasdaq.** ANT opened at \$111.50 ([Nasdaq](https://www.nasdaq.com/newsroom/anthropic-marks-ipo-at-nasdaq)). Nasdaq's data shows a high of \$115.10, a low of \$92.72, a close of \$97.17 (17.07% above the \$83.00 IPO price) and volume of 857,312,300 shares ([Nasdaq](https://www.nasdaq.com/market-activity/stocks/ant/historical)); other outlets printed slightly different volume totals. Nasdaq does not give the time of the first trade. Yahoo Finance reported that it came "nearly noon", with 108 million shares in the opening print ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/article/anthropic-stocks-first-trade-was-worth-12-billion--34-above-its-ipo-price-171721561.html)), and SolanaFloor put it at 11:48 AM ET ([SolanaFloor](https://solanafloor.com/news/anthropics-81-3-b-ipo-breaks-space-x-s-record-as-early-ant-trading-volume-on-solana-tops-29-m)). Both timings are reported.

**IPO allocations through crypto venues.** Bitget said it received a final allocation of 153,774 Anthropic shares (VIP 137,494 at 11.26%; public 16,280 at 1.36%), per Bitget, with unallocated funds to be refunded by the stated UTC deadlines ([Bitget](https://www.bitget.com/support/articles/12560603901105)). xStocks said it "received an allocation" but could "only partially fulfill xStocks IPO Access requests", that funds reserved for unfilled requests would be "released in full" and that no fees were charged ([xStocks on X](https://x.com/xStocksFi/status/2123440937213059208)). Kraken's support article, updated 19 Nov, tells participants to check their email for allocation information and gives no fill rate ([Kraken](https://support.kraken.com/articles/anthropic-ipo-access)). Binance Wallet said the allocation for its campaign was "not ideal relative to total user commitments". It returned all 500,002,764 USDC committed, in two batches, and distributed "roughly 2,957 Anthropic tokenized stocks (ANTB)" pro rata at no cost, plus a \$1,000,000 ANTB airdrop ([Binance](https://www.binance.com/en/support/announcement/detail/f37d1230c2e542fbb1872fcde3ebf140); [Binance Wallet](https://t.me/binance_wallet_announcements/1955)). The campaign was marketed as xStocks ANTx; the posts do not explain how the delivered tokens became ANTB.

**Token launches and prices.** Backpack's ANT went live on Solana through Sunrise at 11:55 AM ET, according to SolanaFloor (reported, [SolanaFloor](https://solanafloor.com/news/anthropics-81-3-b-ipo-breaks-space-x-s-record-as-early-ant-trading-volume-on-solana-tops-29-m)). Securitize announced ANT on Securitize Stocks the same day ([Securitize](https://investors.securitize.io/news/news-details/2026/Securitize-Tokenizes-Anthropic-Stock-Onchain-on-First-Day-of-Public-Trading/default.aspx)), and Robinhood's CEO said its Stock Token was live on Robinhood Chain ([Vlad Tenev on X](https://x.com/vladtenev/status/2123560388318912562)). Solana Compass reported Backpack's ANT at about \$95.50 at 2:22 PM ET against \$95.79 on Nasdaq at 2:00 PM ET ([Solana Compass](https://solanacompass.com/news/tokenized-anthropic-ant-goes-live-on-solana-on-ipo-day-from-backpack-ondo-xstocks-and-securitize)). The two prices are from different times, so they do not measure a premium or discount. No source retrieved gives token prices at the Nasdaq close, so this page states no premium or discount for any token.

**Supply on day 2, before the open (20 Nov, 9:05 AM ET).** Onchain reads show 74,945.7 Backpack ANT on Solana; 540.8 ANTon on Solana and 7,321.9 on Ethereum (229 Ethereum holders, per the Blockscout explorer); 78,355.5 ANTB on BNB Smart Chain; and 15,000,100 ANTx on Solana, the same as before the open on 19 Nov. Supply counts every token in existence, including any an issuer holds, so it is not a measure of investor holdings. Robinhood Chain and the other chains were not read.

**Volume and holders (reported).** SolanaFloor counted more than \$29M of tokenized ANT volume on Solana in about the first 90 minutes: Backpack's ANT \$26.8M, ANTx \$2.22M and ANTon \$39.1K ([SolanaFloor](https://solanafloor.com/news/anthropics-81-3-b-ipo-breaks-space-x-s-record-as-early-ant-trading-volume-on-solana-tops-29-m)). Solana Compass counted more than 8,000 holder accounts for Backpack's ANT and more than 350 for ANTx at 2:22 PM ET ([Solana Compass](https://solanacompass.com/news/tokenized-anthropic-ant-goes-live-on-solana-on-ipo-day-from-backpack-ondo-xstocks-and-securitize)). Uniswap's founder said Uniswap had traded "~83% of all tokenized ANT onchain", after more than \$10M in its first hours ([Hayden Adams on X](https://x.com/haydenzadams/status/2123533850366091566)). Solana's account posted "\$80M+" of ANT volume on Solana by 10:21 PM ET ([Solana on X](https://x.com/solana/status/2123617606754336987)), and Sunrise said ANT passed \$100M on Solana in under 24 hours ([Sunrise on X](https://x.com/sunrise/status/2123769864250695898)). Analyst Tom Wan counted about \$29M of tokenized ANT held onchain on day 2: xStocks \$9.2M, Backpack \$6.0M, bStocks \$5.7M, PreStocks \$5.0M, Robinhood \$2.7M, Ondo \$0.7M ([Tom Wan on X](https://x.com/tomwanhh/status/2123722051823563073)). These figures come from the parties themselves or third parties, use different scopes and cut-off times, and do not reconcile with each other.

**Days 1-2 by chain and by venue (reported, added 21 Nov evening).** Sunrise, which distributes Backpack's ANT, put two-day onchain spot DEX volume (19-20 Nov) at \$140M+ on Solana, \$64M on Robinhood Chain, \$27M on BNB Chain, \$17M on Base and about \$1M on Ethereum, citing Blockworks and GeckoTerminal ([Sunrise on X](https://x.com/sunrise/status/2124251486506885378)).
