ERC-1400 (draft, 2018) is an umbrella suite: ERC-1594 (transfer validation, off-chain data), ERC-1643 (documents), ERC-1644 (controller/forced transfers) and ERC-1410 (partitions) ([spec](https://github.com/SecurityTokenStandard/EIP-Spec/blob/master/eip/eip-1400.md)). ERC-1410 defines balanceOfByPartition, transferByPartition and operator transfers, i.e. "partially fungible" balances useful for lockups and share classes ([ERC-1410](https://github.com/SecurityTokenStandard/EIP-Spec/blob/master/eip/eip-1410.md)). ConsenSys UniversalToken is a reference implementation with certificate-based validation ([GitHub](https://github.com/ConsenSys/ERC1400)).
