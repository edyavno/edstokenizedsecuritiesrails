B20 is the Base-native token standard that Coinbase's tokenized stocks use ([Base](https://blog.base.org/tokenized-stocks); [Base docs](https://docs.base.org/build-on-base/integrate-defi/list-tokenized-stocks)). It is designed to work for any asset; stocks are one use case.

## Design
- A superset of ERC-20, built as Rust precompiles and shipped in Base's Beryl upgrade ([Base docs](https://docs.base.org/upgrades/beryl/b20)). Standard ERC-20 methods and events work.
- Two variants: **Asset** (6-18 decimals, with a multiplier) and **Stablecoin** (6 decimals) ([Base docs](https://docs.base.org/upgrades/beryl/b20)).
- Built-in controls: transfer policies, freeze-and-reissue, roles, memos, supply caps and permit ([Base docs](https://docs.base.org/upgrades/beryl/b20)).
- Audited by Base and Spearbit, per the docs ([Base docs](https://docs.base.org/upgrades/beryl/b20)).

## Tokenized stocks on B20
- **Multiplier**: one token does not always equal one share. Cash dividends are converted to shares and the multiplier rises (e.g. 1.02) ([Base docs](https://docs.base.org/build-on-base/integrate-defi/list-tokenized-stocks)). Scheduled changes use ERC-8056 `updateUIMultiplier`; the instant `updateMultiplier` is deprecated and kept as a failsafe.
- **Identification**: integrators should identify tokens by contract address, not ticker, and watch the `B20Created` event. A public, keyless API returns addresses, supply, multipliers, ISINs and Chainlink reference values ([Base docs](https://docs.base.org/build-on-base/integrate-defi/list-tokenized-stocks)).
- **Access**: secondary transfers are permissionless; KYC applies to Authorized Participants who mint and redeem ([Base docs](https://docs.base.org/build-on-base/integrate-defi/list-tokenized-stocks)).
