*In plain English: ERC-3643 is an Ethereum token standard that only lets approved, identity-checked wallets hold or receive the token.*

ERC-3643 is a final EIP. It extends the basic [[concepts/glossary#erc-20|ERC-20]] token with permissions: transfers can be conditional, paused or frozen, forced, or recovered.

A transfer only goes through if two checks pass ([EIP-3643](https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-3643)):
- the **Identity Registry** confirms both parties hold valid ONCHAINID claims, and
- the modular **Compliance** contract approves it.

Compliance modules cover limits such as country, holder count and balances ([ERC-3643 docs](https://docs.erc3643.org/erc-3643/smart-contracts-library/permissioned-tokens)). Reference implementation: [GitHub](https://github.com/ERC-3643/ERC-3643).
