Final EIP: a permissioned ERC-20 extension with conditional transfers, pause/freeze, forced transfer and recovery. A transfer succeeds only if the **Identity Registry** confirms both parties hold valid ONCHAINID claims and the modular **Compliance** contract approves it ([EIP-3643](https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-3643)). Compliance modules cover limits such as country, holder count and balances ([ERC-3643 docs](https://docs.erc3643.org/erc-3643/smart-contracts-library/permissioned-tokens)). Reference implementation: [GitHub](https://github.com/ERC-3643/ERC-3643).
