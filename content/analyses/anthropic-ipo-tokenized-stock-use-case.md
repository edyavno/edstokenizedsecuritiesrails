**Short answer.** Anthropic sold its IPO shares through underwriters and named brokers. No token was part of that offering. On the first trading day, 19 Nov 2026, products from at least nine issuers or venue groups tracked the stock, and each gives holders a different legal claim ([[comparisons/implementations-by-model|Implementations by model]]). Crypto venues that offered "IPO access" published small or partial allocations: Bitget reported 153,774 shares, per Bitget, and delivered rANT rather than the shares themselves; xStocks could only partly fill requests; Binance Wallet refunded most of the money committed ([Bitget](https://www.bitget.com/support/articles/12560603901105); [xStocks on X](https://x.com/xStocksFi/status/2123440937213059208); [Binance](https://www.binance.com/en/support/announcement/detail/f37d1230c2e542fbb1872fcde3ebf140); [Binance Wallet](https://t.me/binance_wallet_announcements/1955)). At the Nasdaq close on the first two days, the largest token pools traded within about 1% of the stock ([GeckoTerminal](https://www.geckoterminal.com/solana/pools/4v6geaGFiFfQP9HbrxSLdwFKsda8V7jr99ADYq9NtJV1)).

*Updated 21 Nov 2026 with day-one volume by issuer, holdings by issuer, close-time token prices, day-2 data and Bitget rANT (Reality, IPO Prime).*

## Who was allowed to sell Anthropic's IPO shares?

Only underwriters and the brokers Anthropic named.

- The final prospectus (Form 424B4) covers 980,000,000 Class A shares at \$83.00 on Nasdaq under "ANT". It does not mention tokens, blockchains or stablecoins ([424B4](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2130357/000162828026074944/anthropicpbc-424b4.htm)).
- For retail offerings outside the US, Anthropic's Help Center lists authorized brokers and says it "has not authorized any other firm or platform to offer, or to advertise access to, shares in the proposed retail offerings". No crypto venue or tokenizer is on the list ([Claude Help Center](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/17889756-anthropic-ipo-international-retail-brokers-and-selling-agents)).
- An older Help Center page, about private-stock transfers, warns that "tokenized securities" offering Anthropic shares may be an investment that "may have no value" ([Claude Help Center](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/13704655-unauthorized-anthropic-stock-sales-and-investment-scams)).
- Ondo and Securitize say Anthropic did not sponsor or endorse their products, Coinbase's prospectus says the underlying issuer "is not involved in the offer or sale" of its certificates, and Bitget says rANT "is not issued, offered, or managed by Anthropic" and "has not been approved, sponsored, or endorsed by Anthropic" ([Ondo release](https://www.prnewswire.co.uk/news-releases/ondo-stocks-to-launch-anton-with-247-mint-and-redeem-live-on-ipo-day-302943767.html); [Securitize](https://investors.securitize.io/news/news-details/2026/Securitize-Tokenizes-Anthropic-Stock-Onchain-on-First-Day-of-Public-Trading/default.aspx); [Coinbase prospectus](https://www.adgm.com/globalassets/market-disclosure/content/prospectus/coinbase-onchain-spv-ltd---prospectus-antc---18nov2026.pdf); [Bitget](https://www.bitget.com/support/articles/12560603899381)).

One ticker, many different claims.

Most exclude US buyers, and few pass through a vote.

## Did crypto venues get IPO shares?

A little, not much. Relative to the 980,000,000-share offering, even the largest published crypto-venue figure is small.

- **Bitget** said it received a final allocation of 153,774 Anthropic shares (VIP 137,494 at 11.26%; public 16,280 at 1.36%), per Bitget, with unallocated funds to be refunded by 19 Nov 20:00 UTC in the public round and 20 Nov 16:00 UTC in the VIP round ([Bitget](https://www.bitget.com/support/articles/12560603901105)). The delivered asset is rANT, not the shares themselves ([Bitget](https://www.bitget.com/support/articles/12560603899381)).
- **xStocks** "received an allocation" but could "only partially fulfill" requests on Kraken and elsewhere, and released unfilled funds with no fees ([xStocks on X](https://x.com/xStocksFi/status/2123440937213059208); [Kraken](https://support.kraken.com/articles/anthropic-ipo-access)).
- **Binance Wallet** called its allocation "not ideal relative to total user commitments". It returned all 500,002,764 USDC committed and distributed "roughly 2,957" ANTB tokens at no cost, plus a \$1,000,000 ANTB airdrop ([Binance](https://www.binance.com/en/support/announcement/detail/f37d1230c2e542fbb1872fcde3ebf140); [Binance Wallet](https://t.me/binance_wallet_announcements/1955)).
- **Ondo, Dinari and Coinbase** did not take part in the IPO; their products started after trading began ([Ondo release](https://www.prnewswire.co.uk/news-releases/ondo-stocks-to-launch-anton-with-247-mint-and-redeem-live-on-ipo-day-302943767.html); [Dinari FAQ](https://docs.dinari.com/docs/anthropic-ipo-faqs); [Coinbase prospectus](https://www.adgm.com/globalassets/market-disclosure/content/prospectus/coinbase-onchain-spv-ltd---prospectus-antc---18nov2026.pdf)).

## When did each tokenized version of Anthropic go live?

**Short answer.** Tokenized Anthropic came in three waves. A pre-IPO token (PreStocks) and pre-IPO perpetuals traded before the listing. Bitget announced rANT via IPO Prime on 2 Nov ET (3 Nov, 10:00 UTC+8). Between 6 and 18 Nov, Ondo, Dinari, Kraken/xStocks, Backpack, Coinbase, OKX and Binance Wallet also announced products. On 19 Nov, Anthropic's first Nasdaq day, most went live within minutes to hours of the first trade, around 11:48 AM ET. None was part of the IPO itself ([424B4](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2130357/000162828026074944/anthropicpbc-424b4.htm)).

**What the timeline shows**

- **Announced early, live on the day.** Most issuers announced 1 to 13 days ahead and went live on 19 Nov, after the first Nasdaq trade. Backpack's ANT was trading on Solana about seven minutes after it, per SolanaFloor's reported times ([SolanaFloor](https://solanafloor.com/news/anthropics-81-3-b-ipo-breaks-space-x-s-record-as-early-ant-trading-volume-on-solana-tops-29-m)).
- **Allocation and tokenization were separate steps.** Venues that took IPO orders published different outcomes: Bitget reported 153,774 shares, per Bitget; xStocks, via Kraken, got a partial fill; Binance Wallet called its allocation "not ideal" and refunded unfilled money. Ondo, Dinari and Coinbase did not take part in the IPO ([Bitget](https://www.bitget.com/support/articles/12560603901105); [xStocks on X](https://x.com/xStocksFi/status/2123440937213059208); [Binance](https://www.binance.com/en/support/announcement/detail/f37d1230c2e542fbb1872fcde3ebf140); [Ondo release](https://www.prnewswire.co.uk/news-releases/ondo-stocks-to-launch-anton-with-247-mint-and-redeem-live-on-ipo-day-302943767.html); [Dinari FAQ](https://docs.dinari.com/docs/anthropic-ipo-faqs); [Coinbase prospectus](https://www.adgm.com/globalassets/market-disclosure/content/prospectus/coinbase-onchain-spv-ltd---prospectus-antc---18nov2026.pdf)).
- **Exposure came before the listing.** PreStocks and the pre-IPO perps traded first, and neither gives holders a share: PreStocks offers "only economic exposure" and the perps are cash-settled ([PreStocks](https://prestocks.com/anthropic); [trade.xyz](https://docs.trade.xyz/perpetuals/markets/pre-ipo-perpetuals.md)).
- **Not confirmed in the sources retrieved:** Dinari's ANT going live; when OKX's and Kraken's perpetuals converted; rANT's first trade time (Bitget announced a 19 Nov listing during US regular hours, but the retrieved articles do not confirm the open); Reality's legal entity, chain and contract; and whether US persons can hold rANT.

## What does the use case show?

- **The tokens sat outside the IPO.** The share sale ran through underwriters and named brokers; third parties issued the tokens under their own terms ([Anthropic 424B4](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2130357/000162828026074944/anthropicpbc-424b4.htm); [Claude Help Center](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/17889756-anthropic-ipo-international-retail-brokers-and-selling-agents)).
- **"Tokenized Anthropic" is a family of claims.** One ticker covered entitlements, debt securities, certificates, tracker tokens and derivatives ([[concepts/legal-taxonomy|Legal taxonomy]]; [[comparisons/tokenized-stocks-compared|Tokenized stocks compared]]).
- **Prices held close to the stock.** At both closes, the largest Backpack, ANTB and ANTc pools were 0.4% to 1.0% below Nasdaq ([GeckoTerminal](https://www.geckoterminal.com/solana/pools/4v6geaGFiFfQP9HbrxSLdwFKsda8V7jr99ADYq9NtJV1)).
- **Trading and holding are different businesses.** The issuer with the most volume was not the issuer with the most held ([TechFlow](https://www.techflowpost.com/en-US/newsletter/145938); [Tom Wan on X](https://x.com/tomwanhh/status/2123722051823563073)).
- **Check the contract address.** Several tokens use "ANT"; issuers publish their own addresses ([Binance](https://www.binance.com/en/support/announcement/detail/830c320848af4257812d5b6e99e031f9); [Robinhood Final Terms](https://cdn.robinhood.com/assets/robinhood/legal/rhj_final_terms_for_tokenised_debt_securities_linked_to_anthropic_pbc.pdf)).
- **One venue converts between wrappers.** Binance converts eligible third-party tokens into ANTB 1:1 for a limited time, under a daily cap ([Binance](https://www.binance.com/en/support/announcement/detail/830c320848af4257812d5b6e99e031f9)).

## Frequently asked questions

### Could you buy Anthropic's IPO shares as tokens?

Not as part of the IPO. Anthropic's authorized brokers include no tokenizer ([Claude Help Center](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/17889756-anthropic-ipo-international-retail-brokers-and-selling-agents)). Bitget said it received 153,774 shares, per Bitget, and delivered rANT, which it says is not equivalent to holding the shares ([Bitget](https://www.bitget.com/support/articles/12560603901105); [Bitget](https://www.bitget.com/support/articles/12560603899381)). xStocks could only partly fill requests, and Binance Wallet refunded all committed USDC ([xStocks on X](https://x.com/xStocksFi/status/2123440937213059208); [Binance Wallet](https://t.me/binance_wallet_announcements/1955)).

### Is a tokenized Anthropic share the same as a share?

No. Securitize says its holders are not registered shareholders of Anthropic, and Robinhood's Stock Token, xStocks, Ondo's token and Bitget's rANT give no voting rights (Bitget: not automatically) ([Securitize](https://investors.securitize.io/news/news-details/2026/Securitize-Tokenizes-Anthropic-Stock-Onchain-on-First-Day-of-Public-Trading/default.aspx); [Robinhood Final Terms](https://cdn.robinhood.com/assets/robinhood/legal/rhj_final_terms_for_tokenised_debt_securities_linked_to_anthropic_pbc.pdf); [Kraken](https://support.kraken.com/articles/anthropic-ipo-access); [Ondo release](https://www.prnewswire.co.uk/news-releases/ondo-stocks-to-launch-anton-with-247-mint-and-redeem-live-on-ipo-day-302943767.html); [Bitget](https://www.bitget.com/support/articles/12560603899381)).

### Did Anthropic approve any of these tokens?

None of the sources retrieved says so. Ondo and Securitize say Anthropic did not sponsor or endorse their tokens, and Bitget says rANT has no official partnership with Anthropic and has not been approved, sponsored or endorsed by it ([Ondo release](https://www.prnewswire.co.uk/news-releases/ondo-stocks-to-launch-anton-with-247-mint-and-redeem-live-on-ipo-day-302943767.html); [Securitize](https://investors.securitize.io/news/news-details/2026/Securitize-Tokenizes-Anthropic-Stock-Onchain-on-First-Day-of-Public-Trading/default.aspx); [Bitget](https://www.bitget.com/support/articles/12560603899381)).

### Can US residents hold a tokenized Anthropic share?

Only through products open to US persons, such as Securitize's ANT and Dinari's dShares. Robinhood's Stock Token, bStocks, xStocks, Ondo, Coinbase's ANTc and PreStocks exclude US persons ([Securitize](https://investors.securitize.io/news/news-details/2026/Securitize-Tokenizes-Anthropic-Stock-Onchain-on-First-Day-of-Public-Trading/default.aspx); [Dinari FAQ](https://docs.dinari.com/docs/anthropic-ipo-faqs); [Robinhood docs](https://docs.robinhood.com/rhj); [Binance](https://www.binance.com/en/support/announcement/detail/830c320848af4257812d5b6e99e031f9); [Ondo release](https://www.prnewswire.co.uk/news-releases/ondo-stocks-to-launch-anton-with-247-mint-and-redeem-live-on-ipo-day-302943767.html); [Coinbase prospectus](https://www.adgm.com/globalassets/market-disclosure/content/prospectus/coinbase-onchain-spv-ltd---prospectus-antc---18nov2026.pdf); [PreStocks](https://prestocks.com/anthropic)).


### Has Coinbase's ANTc been listed as Aave collateral?

Not as of 23 Nov 2026. On that day, at 3:26 AM ET, LlamaRisk posted an addendum to an 18 Nov Aave ARFC proposing to add ANTc as collateral-only on the Aave V4 Equities Hub on Base, at a 45% collateral factor, a 6.5% maximum liquidation bonus, 10% collateral risk and a $5.00 million add cap. LlamaRisk says ANT had one session of its own trading history when that factor was set, and it lists further early share releases starting 15 Dec 2026. A lender reply the same day asked to delay or stage the listing until after those releases and first earnings. The proposal is not stated as executed ([ARFC, expand Coinbase tokenized equities](https://governance.aave.com/t/arfc-expand-coinbase-tokenized-equities-on-base/26441)). See [[platforms-venues/aave|Aave]] and [[issuers/coinbase-tokenized-stocks#anthropic-antc|Coinbase ANTc]].

### Did the tokens trade at a premium to Nasdaq?

No. In the last minute before the 4:00 PM ET close, the largest pools for Backpack's ANT, ANTB and ANTc traded 0.5% to 0.7% below Nasdaq's \$97.17 close on 19 Nov and 0.4% to 1.0% below its \$88.91 close on 20 Nov ([GeckoTerminal](https://www.geckoterminal.com/solana/pools/4v6geaGFiFfQP9HbrxSLdwFKsda8V7jr99ADYq9NtJV1)). These are single-pool prices, not prices for a large order.
