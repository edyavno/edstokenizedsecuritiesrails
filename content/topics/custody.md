Backed models hold underlying shares with a custodian or broker-dealer (Ondo: US broker-dealers; Dinari: registered US broker-dealer; Coinbase: bankruptcy-remote structure) ([Zerion](https://zerion.io/blog/best-tokenized-stock-platforms/); [Fensory](https://fensory.com/best/tokenized-stocks-platforms)). Issuer-sponsored models replace custody with a transfer-agent register.
