*In plain English: Tokenized stocks come in a few legal forms. Some are the real share; others are a claim or a note that tracks it.*

Tokenized stocks fall into distinct legal models ([Zerion](https://zerion.io/blog/best-tokenized-stock-platforms/)):
- **Debt security / certificate** backed by shares (Robinhood, bStocks, xStocks).
- **Beneficial claim** on custodied shares (Coinbase [[concepts/glossary#b20|B20]]; Securitize Stocks as [[concepts/glossary#security-entitlement|security entitlements]], convertible to registered shares).
- **Issuer-sponsored shares** recorded by a [[concepts/glossary#transfer-agent|transfer agent]] (Securitize, Superstate Opening Bell).
- **Blockchain-native equity** (Figure FGRS).
- **Same security, tokenized form** inside the US market structure (Nasdaq / [[concepts/glossary#dtc|DTC]] pilot, [SEC](https://www.sec.gov/files/rules/sro/nasdaq/2026/34-105047.pdf)).
Rights differ: backing does not automatically confer shareholder rights.

In the [[concepts/glossary#issuer-sponsored-token|issuer-sponsored]] model the token is tied to the company's official shareholder register, so voting, dividend and corporate-action rights carry over; wrapped and synthetic tokens only track the share. Most US shares sit in [[concepts/glossary#street-name|street name]], registered to DTC's nominee [[concepts/glossary#cede-co|Cede & Co.]]; moving them into the holder's own name runs through the [[concepts/glossary#direct-registration-system-drs|Direct Registration System (DRS)]], which DTC has proposed to speed up "from days to minutes" ([SEC notice](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/10/05/2026-20301/self-regulatory-organizations-the-depository-trust-company-notice-of-filing-of-proposed-rule-change)). Since Sep 2026 the [[concepts/issuer-sponsored-token-coalition|Issuer Sponsored Token Coalition]] has pushed this model ([Bullish](https://www.bullish.com/us/news-insights/leading-market-infrastructure-firms-form-coalition-to-advance-issuer-sponsored-tokenized-securities)).
