*In plain English: Where tokenized securities stand under the rules in each place this wiki tracks, in one table.*

Each row gives the status and the main rule or regulator guidance, linked to the regulator's own document. **Not yet collected** means this wiki has no source for that place yet; it does not mean there are no rules. 5 of 11 jurisdictions are not yet collected.

| Jurisdiction | Status | Main rule or guidance | Last checked |
|---|---|---|---|
| United States (federal) | Rules in force | [SEC Innovation Exemption for tokenized NMS stock (Rel. 34-106402)](https://business.cch.com/srd/34-106402091726.pdf) | 2026-10-25 |
| United States (states) | Not yet collected | unknown | not yet checked |
| European Union | Pilot or sandbox | [DLT Pilot Regime (ESMA recommends making it permanent and wider)](https://www.esma.europa.eu/sites/default/files/2025-06/ESMA75-117376770-460_Report_on_the_functioning_and_review_of_the_DLTR_-_Art.14.pdf) | 2026-10-25 |
| United Kingdom | Pilot or sandbox | [Digital Securities Sandbox (Bank of England and FCA)](https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/financial-stability/digital-securities-sandbox) | 2026-10-25 |
| Switzerland | Rules in force | [DLT Act (FinMIA Chapter 4a, DLT trading facilities)](https://www.finma.ch/en/authorisation/fintech/dlt-handelssystem/) | 2026-10-25 |
| Singapore | Regulator guidance | [MAS Guide on the Tokenisation of Capital Markets Products](https://www.mas.gov.sg/regulation/guidelines/guide-on-tokenisation-of-cmps) | 2026-10-25 |
| Hong Kong | Regulator guidance | [SFC circular on tokenised securities-related activities (23EC52)](https://apps.sfc.hk/edistributionWeb/gateway/EN/circular/doc?refNo=23EC52) | 2026-10-25 |
| UAE (ADGM) | Not yet collected | unknown (general regime not collected; bStocks certificates are admitted to the FSRA Official List) | not yet checked |
| UAE (Dubai and federal) | Not yet collected | unknown | not yet checked |
| Jersey | Not yet collected | unknown (issuer domicile for xStocks and Robinhood Stock Tokens) | not yet checked |
| British Virgin Islands | Not yet collected | unknown (issuer domicile for Ondo Global Markets) | not yet checked |

**Status key.** *Rules in force*: a rule, order or licence regime that applies now. *Pilot or sandbox*: a time-limited or capped regime. *Regulator guidance*: guidance on how existing securities law applies to tokens. *Proposed*: filed or proposed, not yet approved.

For the detail behind each row, see [[topics/regulation|Regulation]], [[concepts/innovation-exemption|Innovation Exemption]] and [[concepts/international|International]].
