*In plain English: The main US and international rule changes for tokenized securities, in date order.*

Key US milestones: [[concepts/glossary#dtc|DTC]] [[concepts/glossary#no-action-letter|no-action letter]] (11 Dec 2025) and SEC approval of Nasdaq's tokenized trading rule (18 Mar 2026) ([SEC](https://www.sec.gov/files/rules/sro/nasdaq/2026/34-105047.pdf)); WisdomTree's [[concepts/glossary#exemptive-relief|exemptive relief]] for 24/7 fund-share trading (Feb 2026, [WisdomTree](https://ir.wisdomtree.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/777/wisdomtree-to-launch-247-trading-and-instant-settlement)). Non-US issuers sit in Jersey, BVI and ADGM ([Zerion](https://zerion.io/blog/best-tokenized-stock-platforms/)).

Earlier input: at a 27 Jan 2026 SEC Crypto Task Force meeting, SIFMA (with Citadel and JPMorgan) argued for rulemaking over broad exemptions and split tokenized securities into three models: native (issued by the issuer and its [[concepts/glossary#transfer-agent|transfer agent]]), wrapped (an ADR-like token that is a separate security) and security entitlement tokens (a custodian's record of a customer's holding) ([SEC memo](https://www.sec.gov/files/ctf-memo-sifma-cahill-gordon-reindel-llp-citadel-llc-jpmorgan-chase-co-012726.pdf)).

Recent changes (Sep to Oct 2026): the SEC's [[concepts/innovation-exemption|Innovation Exemption]] for tokenized NMS stock (17 Sep 2026, [SEC](https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/press-releases/2026-90-sec-issues-innovation-exemption-facilitate-trading-tokenized-nms-stock-request-comment)), which Better Markets' 22 Oct comment letter urges the SEC to withdraw in favor of notice-and-comment rulemaking ([Better Markets](https://bettermarkets.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Better-Markets-Comment-Letter-SEC-Innovation-Exemption.pdf)), while SIFMA's 19 Oct letter asks for amendments such as issuer opt-in consent and a 90-day objection period ([SIFMA](https://www.sifma.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/SIFMA-Innovation-Exemption-Initial-Letter-10-19-2026.pdf)), Solana Policy Institute's 22 Oct letter backs the order and asks the SEC to define "public, permissionless" ledgers by who controls them ([Solana Policy Institute](https://www.sec.gov/comments/4-927/4927-1106380-3893468.pdf)), and Malinova and Park (23 Oct) argue the volume caps block arbitrage and raise trading costs ([Malinova and Park](https://www.sec.gov/comments/4-927/4927-1107499-3908312.pdf)); CFTC staff FAQs on tokenized permitted investments (24 Sep 2026, [CFTC](https://www.cftc.gov/PressRoom/PressReleases/9303-26)); the OCC's final GENIUS Act rule, effective 18 Jan 2027 (21 Oct 2026, [OCC](https://www.occ.gov/news-issuances/bulletins/2026/bulletin-2026-50.html)); the FCA and Bank of England feedback statement FS26/1 (14 Sep 2026, [FCA](https://www.fca.org.uk/publications/feedback-statements/fs26-1-tokenisation-wholesale-markets)); and EU Council agreement on key elements of the market integration package, which would widen the DLT Pilot Regime (9 Oct 2026, [Council](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/press/press-releases/2026/10/09/savings-and-investments-union-council-agrees-key-elements-of-crucial-measures-to-deepen-eu-capital-markets/)).

Country-by-country status: [[topics/rules-by-jurisdiction|Rules by jurisdiction]].

Outside the US (Sep to Nov 2026): South Korea published its tokenization roadmap (4 Sep 2026, [FSC](https://fsc.go.kr/eng/pr010101/87653)) and draft rules for the regime that starts 4 Feb 2027, with comments open to 11 Nov 2026 ([FSC](https://fsc.go.kr/eng/pr010101/87834)); the Reserve Bank of Australia consulted on settling tokenised assets in central bank money (3 Sep 2026, [RBA](https://www.rba.gov.au/payments-and-infrastructure/tokenised-money/the-role-of-rits-in-supporting-settlement-in-a-tokenised-ecosystem/pdf/the-role-of-rits-in-supporting-settlement-in-a-tokenised-ecosystem.pdf)).
