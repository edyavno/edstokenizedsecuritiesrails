*In plain English: The main US and international rule changes for tokenized securities, in date order.*

Key US milestones: [[concepts/glossary#dtc|DTC]] [[concepts/glossary#no-action-letter|no-action letter]] (11 Dec 2025) and SEC approval of Nasdaq's tokenized trading rule (18 Mar 2026) ([SEC](https://www.sec.gov/files/rules/sro/nasdaq/2026/34-105047.pdf)); WisdomTree's [[concepts/glossary#exemptive-relief|exemptive relief]] for 24/7 fund-share trading (Feb 2026, [WisdomTree](https://ir.wisdomtree.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/777/wisdomtree-to-launch-247-trading-and-instant-settlement)). Non-US issuers sit in Jersey, BVI and ADGM ([Zerion](https://zerion.io/blog/best-tokenized-stock-platforms/)).

Recent changes (Sep to Oct 2026): the SEC's [[concepts/innovation-exemption|Innovation Exemption]] for tokenized NMS stock (17 Sep 2026, [SEC](https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/press-releases/2026-90-sec-issues-innovation-exemption-facilitate-trading-tokenized-nms-stock-request-comment)); CFTC staff FAQs on tokenized permitted investments (24 Sep 2026, [CFTC](https://www.cftc.gov/PressRoom/PressReleases/9303-26)); the OCC's final GENIUS Act rule, effective 18 Jan 2027 (21 Oct 2026, [OCC](https://www.occ.gov/news-issuances/bulletins/2026/bulletin-2026-50.html)); the FCA and Bank of England feedback statement FS26/1 (14 Sep 2026, [FCA](https://www.fca.org.uk/publications/feedback-statements/fs26-1-tokenisation-wholesale-markets)); and EU Council agreement on key elements of the market integration package, which would widen the DLT Pilot Regime (9 Oct 2026, [Council](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/press/press-releases/2026/10/09/savings-and-investments-union-council-agrees-key-elements-of-crucial-measures-to-deepen-eu-capital-markets/)).
