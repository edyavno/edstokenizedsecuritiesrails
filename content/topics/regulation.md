*In plain English: The main US and international rule changes for tokenized securities, in date order.*

Key US milestones: [[concepts/glossary#dtc|DTC]] [[concepts/glossary#no-action-letter|no-action letter]] (11 Dec 2025) and SEC approval of Nasdaq's tokenized trading rule (18 Mar 2026) ([SEC](https://www.sec.gov/files/rules/sro/nasdaq/2026/34-105047.pdf)); WisdomTree's [[concepts/glossary#exemptive-relief|exemptive relief]] for 24/7 fund-share trading (Feb 2026, [WisdomTree](https://ir.wisdomtree.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/777/wisdomtree-to-launch-247-trading-and-instant-settlement)). Non-US issuers sit in Jersey, BVI and ADGM ([Zerion](https://zerion.io/blog/best-tokenized-stock-platforms/)).

Earlier input: at a 27 Jan 2026 SEC Crypto Task Force meeting, SIFMA (with Citadel and JPMorgan) argued for rulemaking over broad exemptions and split tokenized securities into three models: native (issued by the issuer and its [[concepts/glossary#transfer-agent|transfer agent]]), wrapped (an ADR-like token that is a separate security) and security entitlement tokens (a custodian's record of a customer's holding) ([SEC memo](https://www.sec.gov/files/ctf-memo-sifma-cahill-gordon-reindel-llp-citadel-llc-jpmorgan-chase-co-012726.pdf)).

Recent changes (Sep to Oct 2026): the SEC's [[concepts/innovation-exemption|Innovation Exemption]] for tokenized NMS stock (17 Sep 2026, [SEC](https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/press-releases/2026-90-sec-issues-innovation-exemption-facilitate-trading-tokenized-nms-stock-request-comment)), which Better Markets' 22 Oct comment letter urges the SEC to withdraw in favor of notice-and-comment rulemaking ([Better Markets](https://bettermarkets.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Better-Markets-Comment-Letter-SEC-Innovation-Exemption.pdf)), while SIFMA's 19 Oct letter asks for amendments such as issuer opt-in consent and a 90-day objection period ([SIFMA](https://www.sifma.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/SIFMA-Innovation-Exemption-Initial-Letter-10-19-2026.pdf)), Solana Policy Institute's 22 Oct letter backs the order and asks the SEC to define "public, permissionless" ledgers by who controls them ([Solana Policy Institute](https://www.sec.gov/comments/4-927/4927-1106380-3893468.pdf)), and Malinova and Park (23 Oct) argue the volume caps block arbitrage and raise trading costs ([Malinova and Park](https://www.sec.gov/comments/4-927/4927-1107499-3908312.pdf)); Shadow SEC Statement No. 17, filed 10 Nov 2026 (statement dated 19 Oct) by five securities-law scholars including former SEC General Counsel John C. Coates, argues that the order is a legislative rule in substance that should have gone through APA notice and comment, and that it will fragment liquidity because it does not require connectivity between TSVs or aggregation of quotes or last-sale information ([Shadow SEC Statement No. 17](https://www.sec.gov/comments/4-927/4927-1131060-4029687.pdf)); CFTC staff FAQs on tokenized permitted investments (24 Sep 2026, [CFTC](https://www.cftc.gov/PressRoom/PressReleases/9303-26)); the OCC's final GENIUS Act rule, effective 18 Jan 2027 (21 Oct 2026, [OCC](https://www.occ.gov/news-issuances/bulletins/2026/bulletin-2026-50.html)); the FCA and Bank of England feedback statement FS26/1 (14 Sep 2026, [FCA](https://www.fca.org.uk/publications/feedback-statements/fs26-1-tokenisation-wholesale-markets)); and EU Council agreement on key elements of the market integration package, which would widen the DLT Pilot Regime (9 Oct 2026, [Council](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/press/press-releases/2026/10/09/savings-and-investments-union-council-agrees-key-elements-of-crucial-measures-to-deepen-eu-capital-markets/)).

November 2026 signals: IOSCO's 2026 monitoring report calls tokenization "transitional and fragmented" and asks regulators to tell tokenized shares that carry shareholder rights apart from products that only give price exposure ([IOSCO 2026](https://www.iosco.org/library/pubdocs/pdf/IOSCOPD832.pdf)). Four Senate Democrats (Alsobrooks, Warner, Gallego and Cortez Masto) wrote to Chairman Atkins on 13 Nov 2026 asking 13 questions about the [[concepts/innovation-exemption|Innovation Exemption]], including AML/KYC, token-level transfer restrictions and issuer opt-outs, and urging notice-and-comment rulemaking ([letter](https://www.alsobrooks.senate.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Alsobrooks_Warner_Cortez_Masto_Gallego_SEC_Innovation_Exemption_Tokenization_Letter_11-12-2026_@_02-10_PM.pdf)). On 16 Nov 2026 SEC Trading and Markets Director Jamie Selway, speaking for himself, said a permanent rule proposal has "got to be a 2027 event" ([Unchained livestream at 1:10:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mwAidpXpJJ4&t=4255s)). Fed Governor Christopher Waller said on 17 Nov 2026 that atomic settlement should not be the only model for tokenized markets ([Federal Reserve](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/speech/waller20261117a.htm); see [[topics/settlement-and-matching|Settlement & matching]]). On the derivatives side, the CFTC set a 15 Dec 2026 roundtable on 24/7 trading whose agenda asks how tokenization and stablecoins could address roadblocks to 24/7 clearing ([CFTC](https://www.cftc.gov/PressRoom/PressReleases/9327-26); [agenda](https://www.cftc.gov/media/14941/RoundtablePublicFacing_Agenda121526/download)).

Transfer agent rules (proposed, Sep 2026): the SEC proposed amendments to its transfer agent rules (Release 34-106246, 1 Sep 2026, 91 FR 56946, File S7-2026-30), as cited in [Nasdaq's comment letter](https://www.sec.gov/comments/S7-2026-30/s7202630-1122619-3995826.pdf). Comment letters are advocacy, not law, and they diverge: Nasdaq (5 Nov) asks for interoperability between onchain and offchain master securityholder files and the national clearance and settlement system, and fungibility at the recordkeeping layer ([Nasdaq](https://www.sec.gov/comments/S7-2026-30/s7202630-1122619-3995826.pdf)); Coinbase (3 Nov) asks that holders be identifiable by wallet address without a mandatory name and mailing address, and that a blockchain record can be the master file ([Coinbase](https://www.sec.gov/comments/S7-2026-30/s7202630-1119719-3983946.pdf)); [[infrastructure/fairmint|Fairmint]] (3 Nov) proposes one recordkeeping transfer agent with co-transfer agents per venue and a functional test of "exclusive control" ([Fairmint](https://www.sec.gov/comments/S7-2026-30/s7202630-1120639-3987647.pdf)); Metrika (3 Nov) addresses keeping the master securityholder file on a public chain the transfer agent does not control, and asks the SEC to treat network risk as a material risk under Rule 17ad-12 calling for real-time monitoring, to treat exclusive control as covering the token's privileged functions (issue, cancel, freeze, force-transfer) and the off-chain address-to-holder mapping, to treat an item as posted when it reaches finality under the agent's documented standard, and to have Form TA-2 name the networks used ([Metrika letter](https://www.sec.gov/comments/S7-2026-30/s7202630-1119921-3984266.pdf)). The network-incident figures in that letter, including a 12 Aug 2026 Solana finality stall (roughly 29 percent of staked SOL offline; no blocks finalized for roughly half an hour) and five Solana protocol changes between 21 Aug and 21 Oct 2026, are Metrika's monitoring data. An official onchain register is already possible today (for example CNTN's); the name and mailing address baseline is the proposal's. Other letters, including from the Blockchain Association and the Crypto Council for Innovation, which Coinbase endorses, are on the [SEC comment page](https://www.sec.gov/rules-regulations/public-comments/s7-2026-30). Detail: [[build/trading-reference-architecture#holder-rights-and-compliance-controls|Holder rights and compliance controls]].

Country-by-country status: [[topics/rules-by-jurisdiction|Rules by jurisdiction]].

Outside the US (Sep to Nov 2026): South Korea published its tokenization roadmap (4 Sep 2026, [FSC](https://fsc.go.kr/eng/pr010101/87653)) and draft rules for the regime that starts 4 Feb 2027, with comments open to 11 Nov 2026 ([FSC](https://fsc.go.kr/eng/pr010101/87834)); the Reserve Bank of Australia consulted on settling tokenised assets in central bank money (3 Sep 2026, [RBA](https://www.rba.gov.au/payments-and-infrastructure/tokenised-money/the-role-of-rits-in-supporting-settlement-in-a-tokenised-ecosystem/pdf/the-role-of-rits-in-supporting-settlement-in-a-tokenised-ecosystem.pdf)). In the UK, on 18 Nov 2026 the Wholesale Digital Markets Champion welcomed an enabling amendment the government had laid to the Financial Services and Markets Bill, and said settlement finality and Companies Act register rules still need greater certainty before digitally native equities can be issued ([letter](https://www.theglobalcity.uk/PositiveWebsite/media/research-downloads/20261118_CW-Letter.pdf)).
