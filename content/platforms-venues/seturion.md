*In plain English: Seturion lets existing European trading venues keep matching orders as they do today and settle tokenized trades on a blockchain, without each venue needing its own DLT licence.*

On 20 Oct 2026 Boerse Stuttgart Group, which started Seturion, obtained a DLT Trading and Settlement System licence under the EU [[concepts/glossary#dlt-pilot-regime|DLT Pilot Regime]], granted by BaFin after a process with ESMA and the ECB ([Boerse Stuttgart](https://group.boerse-stuttgart.com/en/seturion-blog/boerse-stuttgart-group-obtains-dlt-tss-license-and-lays-regulatory-basis-for-seturion/)). Venues that connect can offer tokenized assets "without requiring their own DLT license" ([Boerse Stuttgart](https://group.boerse-stuttgart.com/en/seturion-blog/boerse-stuttgart-group-obtains-dlt-tss-license-and-lays-regulatory-basis-for-seturion/)).

## How it works
- Bridges offchain trading and onchain settlement; assets stay in participants' own wallet infrastructure, and no central securities depository is needed, per Seturion ([Seturion](https://group.boerse-stuttgart.com/en/seturion/)).
- Supports public and selected private chains, and settlement against central bank money/fiat or onchain cash ([Seturion](https://group.boerse-stuttgart.com/en/seturion/)).
- Already used by SeturionX, a Swiss DLT trading facility ([Seturion](https://group.boerse-stuttgart.com/en/seturion/)).
- Strategic partners named: flatexDEGIRO, Nasdaq, Société Générale and SG-FORGE ([Boerse Stuttgart](https://group.boerse-stuttgart.com/en/seturion-blog/boerse-stuttgart-group-obtains-dlt-tss-license-and-lays-regulatory-basis-for-seturion/)).

See [[build/trading-reference-architecture#how-venues-match-and-settle|How venues match and settle]] and [[concepts/international|International]].

## What this means for holders
- Holders would reach Seturion through a connected venue or broker, not directly ([Boerse Stuttgart](https://group.boerse-stuttgart.com/en/seturion-blog/boerse-stuttgart-group-obtains-dlt-tss-license-and-lays-regulatory-basis-for-seturion/)).
