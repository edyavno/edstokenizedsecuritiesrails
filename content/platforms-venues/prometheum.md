*In plain English: Prometheum lets ordinary US brokers offer tokenized securities by clearing and holding them for those brokers, under the same customer-protection rules as stocks.*

Prometheum Capital, a FINRA member and SEC-registered [[concepts/glossary#broker-dealer|broker-dealer]], offers omnibus and fully disclosed correspondent clearing so other broker-dealers can offer crypto assets, including tokenized and digitally native securities, subject to Customer Protection [[concepts/glossary#rule-15c3-3|Rule 15c3-3]] ([Prometheum](https://www.prometheum.com/correspondent-clearing)).

## How it works
- Prometheum Capital provides clearing, custody and settlement; the introducing broker keeps the customer ([Prometheum](https://www.prometheum.com/correspondent-clearing)).
- The Prometheum [[concepts/glossary#ats|ATS]] "does not trade against its customers or make markets"; customer funds and blockchain securities are kept separate under 15c3-3 ([Prometheum ATS](https://www.prometheum.com/trading)).
- Prometheum describes ATS settlement as instant (T+0) and offers a WebSocket trade API ([Prometheum ATS](https://www.prometheum.com/trading)).

This is the US broker-dealer-native model: eligibility, custody and books sit with a regulated broker rather than in the token. See [[concepts/legal-taxonomy|Legal taxonomy]] and [[comparisons/trading-venue-architectures|Trading venue architectures]].

## What this means for holders
- You would hold through a broker-dealer account, with 15c3-3 segregation, not in a self-custodied wallet ([Prometheum ATS](https://www.prometheum.com/trading)).
