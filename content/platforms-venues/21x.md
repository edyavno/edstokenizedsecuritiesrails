*In plain English: 21X runs a regulated EU market where orders are matched and settled by smart contracts on the blockchain itself, not on a private server.*

21X AG says it was the first venue licensed under the EU [[concepts/glossary#dlt-pilot-regime|DLT Pilot Regime]] to run both trading and settlement, opening its DLT Trading and Settlement System in September 2025 under BaFin and ESMA oversight ([21X](https://21x.eu/21x-ag-and-layerzero-deliver-institutional-grade-onchain-trading-pilot-on-private-permissioned-deployment-of-zero/)). The system combines a DLT multilateral trading facility and a DLT settlement system ([21X docs](https://docs.21x.eu/participant-docs/participant-docs)).

## How it works
- **Matching:** a fully onchain central limit order book. Order entry, matching and settlement run on the blockchain for security-token-to-[[concepts/glossary#stablecoin|stablecoin]] pairs, with deterministic matching and [[concepts/glossary#atomic-settlement|atomic settlement]] ([21X](https://21x.eu/smart-contracts-and-the-role-of-regulated-market-infrastructures/)).
- **Access:** only whitelisted wallets can interact with the contracts. Admin keys use multi-signature protection, and an emergency circuit breaker can pause the system ([21X](https://21x.eu/smart-contracts-and-the-role-of-regulated-market-infrastructures/)).
- **Participants:** issuers, listing sponsors, trade participants, orderflow providers, market makers and liquidity providers, and stablecoin issuers ([21X docs](https://docs.21x.eu/participant-docs/participant-docs)).

## Zero pilot (Nov 2026)
On 4 Nov 2026 21X and LayerZero reported a month-long pilot on US equity tokens that ran 21X's order book on a private-permissioned instance of LayerZero's Zero chain with no offchain matching. **Company-reported figures:** 10,000 order matches per second, "up to a 45x improvement" over the L2 EVM baseline, with matching and settlement in the same transaction ([21X](https://21x.eu/21x-ag-and-layerzero-deliver-institutional-grade-onchain-trading-pilot-on-private-permissioned-deployment-of-zero/); [LayerZero](https://layerzero.network/blog/21x-zero-trading-pilot)). It was a pilot; 21X keeps operating its live venue on its existing chains alongside it ([21X](https://21x.eu/21x-ag-and-layerzero-deliver-institutional-grade-onchain-trading-pilot-on-private-permissioned-deployment-of-zero/)).

How this compares with other venue designs: [[build/trading-reference-architecture#how-venues-match-and-settle|How venues match and settle]] and [[comparisons/trading-venue-architectures|Trading venue architectures]].

## What this means for holders
- Trading happens only between whitelisted wallets, and each trade settles at once against a stablecoin ([21X](https://21x.eu/smart-contracts-and-the-role-of-regulated-market-infrastructures/)).
- The throughput numbers come from 21X and LayerZero and were not independently tested ([21X](https://21x.eu/21x-ag-and-layerzero-deliver-institutional-grade-onchain-trading-pilot-on-private-permissioned-deployment-of-zero/)).
