The first public TSV notice under the [[concepts/innovation-exemption|Innovation Exemption]], dated 4 Oct 2026 ([OKXICE notice](https://www.okx.com/cdn/assets/files/2610/798CC1267CB56FB2.pdf)). Design per the notice: Uniswap v4 pools with an OKXICE TSV hook, access via soulbound tokens, pairs against USDC/USDG/USDT, no order book and no custody; it describes admin-key governance. The notice lists 63 symbols ([Dida Labs](https://didalabs.com/tokenized-stock-objection/), analysis). Dida Labs notes Cerebras is not among the 63 ([Dida Labs](https://didalabs.com/tokenized-stock-objection/)); OKXICE's own notices list it as an objector (see below). Coverage: [Ledger Insights](https://www.ledgerinsights.com/ice-okx-venture-plans-trading-of-tokenized-us-stocks-via-uniswap-pools/), [Crypto Briefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/okx-ice-uniswap-v4-hooks-tokenized-stocks/) (news).

## Issuer objections
OKXICE's public notice of 23 Oct 2026 lists **22** issuers that objected: Cerebras (CBRS), AppLovin, Applied Digital, Rocket Lab, CoreWeave, Verizon, Applied Optoelectronics, Broadcom, UnitedHealth, Goldman Sachs, Microsoft, Qualcomm, Caterpillar, McDonald's, Western Digital, Alphabet (GOOG/GOOGL), Arm, Wells Fargo, Moderna, Cisco, Intuit and Marvell ([OKXICE notice, 23 Oct](https://www.okx.com/cdn/assets/okfe/web3-activities/okxicetsv/notices/OKXICE_Tokenized_Securities_Venue_Public_Notice_26.10.23.pdf)). The first 16 were already on its 19 Oct notice; Arm, Wells Fargo, Moderna, Cisco, Intuit and Marvell are new ([OKXICE notice, 19 Oct](https://www.okx.com/cdn/assets/okfe/web3-activities/okxicetsv/notices/OKXICE_Tokenized_Securities_Venue_Public_Notice_26.10.19.pdf)). CNBC's 21 Oct count of 16 matches the 19 Oct notice ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/21/tokenized-stocks-us-companies-object.html), news). Cerebras appears as an objector even though it was not among the 63 symbols in the 4 Oct notice; neither notice says why (possibly an objection filed ahead of a listing; unverified). Under the [[concepts/innovation-exemption|Innovation Exemption]] the venue must notify each issuer and wait 30 days after receipt before trading ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/21/tokenized-stocks-us-companies-object.html)).
