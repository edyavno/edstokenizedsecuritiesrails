*In plain English: Bitget ran an Anthropic IPO subscription through IPO Prime and said it would list rANT, a Reality token, for spot trading on 19 Nov 2026 during US market hours.*

Bitget is a crypto exchange. The retrieved sources cover only its Anthropic IPO Prime offering and the rANT spot listing, not Bitget's wider business.

## IPO Prime

IPO Prime is "Bitget's global asset subscription platform, focused on investment opportunities in companies before listing and during their initial public offering (IPO)." For the IPO section, Bitget says "subscriptions are settled at the official issue price, and allocated assets are delivered as rTokens backed by real shares" ([Bitget IPO Prime](https://www.bitget.com/spotlight/ipo-prime)).

The Anthropic round, announced 3 Nov 2026 (10:00 UTC+8), had a VIP round (eligible VIP1–VIP7 users; $100 to $3,000,000 per person; $1,000,000,000 total commit cap; 12% underwriting fee) and a public round (all eligible users; $100 to $25,000; $200,000,000 cap; 5% fee), settled in USDT or USDGO at the final IPO issue price plus applicable fees, allocated pro rata, with the total commitment cap "not represent[ing] the platform's allocation" ([Bitget](https://www.bitget.com/support/articles/12560603899381)). Bitget says the product is available only to eligible users in permitted regions and "is not offered to residents of restricted jurisdictions." It does not name those regions in the announcement, including whether US persons can take part.

On 19 Nov 2026 at 17:10 UTC+8 (4:10 AM ET), Bitget said it had "received a final allocation of 153,774 Anthropic shares": VIP 137,494 shares at 11.26%, public 16,280 shares at 1.36%, with public-round distribution and refunds by 19 Nov 20:00 UTC and VIP by 20 Nov 16:00 UTC, per Bitget ([Bitget](https://www.bitget.com/support/articles/12560603901105)). Those figures are Bitget's.

## rANT listing

Bitget said it "will list the stock spot asset rANT on November 19, 2026, with trading opening during US regular market hours" ([Bitget](https://www.bitget.com/support/articles/12560603901016)). The earlier IPO Prime announcement had expected trading to open "about one hour after the underlying stock completes its Nasdaq IPO Cross (opening auction)," subject to change. The retrieved articles do not confirm the first trade time. Bitget's rANT page describes a rANT/USDT spot pair ([Bitget rANT page](https://www.bitget.com/price/anthropic-tokenized-stock-reality)). The issuer Bitget names is [[issuers/reality|Reality]].

See [[comparisons/implementations-by-model|Implementations by model]] and [[analyses/anthropic-ipo-tokenized-stock-use-case|Anthropic's IPO as a tokenized-stock use case]].

## What this means for holders
- IPO Prime delivered a token (rANT), not Anthropic shares, per Bitget ([Bitget](https://www.bitget.com/support/articles/12560603899381)).
- Bitget published a 153,774-share allocation and said unallocated subscription funds would be refunded by the stated UTC deadlines ([Bitget](https://www.bitget.com/support/articles/12560603901105)).
