*In plain English: Kraken is a crypto exchange that lists xStocks for trading.*

Kraken listed xStocks on 30 June 2025 with 24/5 trading and withdrawals ([PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/backeds-xstocks-go-live-today-on-bybit-kraken-and-solana-defi-302494374.html)).

On 18 Aug 2026 Kraken opened 7,000+ US-listed stocks to eligible EEA customers under MiFID II through Payward Europe Digital Solutions (CY) Limited, next to 700+ xStocks in the same account ([Kraken](https://blog.kraken.com/product/equities/announcing-us-listed-stock-trading-for-eea-customers)). EEA clients must pass a questionnaire and can trade xStocks only with fiat via Convert, not on Pro order books or the API ([Kraken support](https://support.kraken.com/articles/xstocks-availability)).

Kraken said on 22 Oct 2026 that its xStocks lineup exceeds 1,000 tokenized instruments ([The Defiant](https://thedefiant.io/news/cefi/kraken-says-its-xstocks-lineup-now-exceeds-1-000), news; not yet confirmed on a Kraken page). A Payward press release on 11 Nov 2026 said a first batch of tokenized UK and EU-listed equities is available to trade across Kraken from day one ([Payward](https://www.payward.com/press-release/xstocks-uk-eu-equities)); see [[issuers/xstocks-backed|xStocks]].

On 12 Nov 2026 Kraken's support pages described "IPO Access" for Anthropic: eligible clients outside the US, UK, Canada, Japan, Australia and New Zealand submit interest in ANTx (an xStocks token) at the offering price plus a 5% spread, with allocation "decided by the IPO underwriters" and refunds for unallocated funds ([Kraken](https://support.kraken.com/articles/anthropic-ipo-access)). Kraken also lists pre-IPO perpetual futures on Anthropic (PF_ANTHROPICXUSD, settled 13 Nov; PF_ANTXUSD, to convert to an xStock perpetual "once there is a viable index") that give no right to shares or tokenized equities ([Kraken](https://support.kraken.com/articles/pre-ipo-perpetual-futures-faq)). See [[comparisons/implementations-by-model|Implementations by model]].

On 19 Nov 2026 xStocks said it could only partially fill IPO Access requests, releasing reserved funds for unfilled requests with no fees ([xStocks on X](https://x.com/xStocksFi/status/2123440937213059208)); Kraken's updated support article tells participants to check their email for allocation information and gives no fill rate ([Kraken](https://support.kraken.com/articles/anthropic-ipo-access)).

On 18 Nov 2026 Payward, Inc. said it had entered into a definitive agreement to acquire Tribe Capital Management, Inc., a venture capital and asset manager with \$2.7 billion in assets under management; Tribe is expected to operate within Payward's Asset Management pillar, and Payward said it plans to develop private-market offerings that connect investment origination and asset management with capabilities across custody, trading, collateral, and tokenization. The transaction is expected to close in the first quarter of 2027, subject to customary closing conditions including required consents; financial terms were not disclosed, and no tokenized product was announced ([Payward](https://www.payward.com/press-release/payward-to-acquire-tribe-capital)). CNBC reported that Payward co-CEO Arjun Sethi is also chairman and co-founder of Tribe ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/19/acquisition-of-tribe-capital-flags-new-chapter-in-evolution-of-crypto-exchanges.html)).

## What this means for holders
- Kraken lists xStocks, so what you hold is an xStock with Backed's terms: see [[issuers/xstocks-backed|xStocks]].
- Trading is 24/5 and tokens can be withdrawn to a self-custody [[concepts/glossary#wallet|wallet]] ([PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/backeds-xstocks-go-live-today-on-bybit-kraken-and-solana-defi-302494374.html)).
