*In plain English: Securitize acts as transfer agent and tokenization platform for funds and companies that issue tokens.*

Example: Exodus Class A common stock tokens on Algorand, with Securitize as [[concepts/glossary#transfer-agent|transfer agent]] ([PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/exodus-issues-security-token-on-algorand-expanding-access-to-the-growing-digital-security-ecosystem-301304582.html)); trading began on Securitize Markets in March 2022 ([Securitize](https://securitize.io/press-releases/exodus-begins-trading-on-securitize-markets)).

On 17 Nov 2026 Securitize said it had completed its acquisition of River One (River Credit Labs Inc.), a New York asset-backed warehouse lender founded in 2025 that aims to connect stablecoin capital with traditional lending markets; it is Securitize's first acquisition since becoming a public company in July 2026, and the release says River One's Harbor framework could bring structured credit products onchain for distribution through Securitize's existing infrastructure ([Securitize](https://investors.securitize.io/news/news-details/2026/Securitize-Completes-Acquisition-of-River-One-Expanding-Into-6-Trillion-Asset-Backed-Finance-Market/default.aspx)).

## What are Securitize Stocks?
Securitize Stocks, launched on 8 Oct 2026, are UCC Article 8 security entitlements to US stocks, 1:1 backed, held through Securitize's broker-dealer.

They launched on [[concepts/glossary#solana|Solana]], settled in USDC, through Securitize's [[concepts/glossary#broker-dealer|broker-dealer]]; Jump makes markets on the PropAMM; conversion to direct registration where available; an NYSE venue was planned in that launch release ([PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/securitize-launches-global-onchain-trading-of-us-stocks-with-security-entitlements-302902107.html); [RQD](https://rqdclearing.com/securitize-launches-global-onchain-trading-of-u-s-stocks-with-security-entitlements/)). The structure is called a convertible [[concepts/glossary#entitlement-token|entitlement token]] (CET). Securitize says it is open to eligible investors in the US, EU and other permitted jurisdictions, the backing shares will not be lent out, and lending and collateral use on markets like Aave is planned ([PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/securitize-launches-global-onchain-trading-of-us-stocks-with-security-entitlements-302902107.html)). CoinDesk reported 12 companies at launch ([CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/business/2026/10/08/securitize-brings-apple-nvidia-and-tesla-to-solana-with-nyse-trading-in-the-works), news). A 2 Nov 2026 Securitize release says a second wave adds 13 security-entitlement tokens on Solana, bringing the total offering to 25 stocks for eligible investors in the United States, the European Union and other permitted jurisdictions. The 13 names added are Arista Networks (ANET), Flex Ltd. (FLEX), Exxon Mobil (XOM), GameStop (GME), Johnson & Johnson (JNJ), Lockheed Martin (LMT), Lumentum (LITE), Merck & Co. (MRK), Salesforce (CRM), Seagate Technology (STX), Texas Instruments (TXN), The Walt Disney Company (DIS) and Uber Technologies (UBER). The release says those join the original 12 launched in October: AAPL, MSFT, NVDA, GOOG, TSLA, META, AMZN, NFLX, CRCL, SPCX, MSTR and PLTR ([Securitize](https://investors.securitize.io/news/news-details/2026/Securitize-Expands-Onchain-U-S--Stock-Offering-to-25-Equities/default.aspx)).

Securitize's Q3 2026 10-Q says its broker-dealer, Securitize Markets, received FINRA approval to custody tokenized securities after SEC staff's December 2025 custody statement ([10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2094496/000162828026073910/secz-20260930.htm)).

On 16 Nov 2026, Apple's dividend pay date, Securitize paid that dividend in USDC on Solana to holders of its Apple token; see [[topics/corporate-actions-and-dividends#securitize-apple-dividend-16-nov-2026|Corporate actions]] for the reported figures ([Bitquery](https://bitquery.io/investigations/securitize-apple-dividend-onchain); [Carlos Domingo on X](https://x.com/carlosdomingo/status/2123822462194643349)).

On 11 Nov 2026 OKX said Securitize will provide the tokenization infrastructure for [[platforms-venues/okxice-tsv|OKXICE TSV]], with trading due to begin on 23 Nov 2026. Each token on that venue will represent a security entitlement backed one-to-one by an underlying U.S. share, with applicable shareholder rights including dividends, voting and corporate actions ([OKX](https://www.okx.com/en-us/learn/okxice-tsv-launch)). That is a tokenizer role for the TSV, distinct from Securitize Stocks on Solana.

## Anthropic (ANT) on Securitize Stocks (19 Nov 2026)
On 19 Nov 2026, Anthropic's first day of trading, Securitize said Anthropic (Nasdaq: ANT) was available through Securitize Stocks on Solana, alongside new additions Amgen and Fox, bringing the offering to more than 50 U.S. equities. It describes the tokens as "security entitlements, not shares registered directly in the holder's name, backed 1:1 by corresponding underlying securities held through Securitize Markets' brokerage infrastructure", offered through Securitize Markets, LLC, a FINRA/SIPC member broker-dealer, to "eligible investors in the United States, European Union (EU) and other permitted jurisdictions". "Rights" means "applicable economic benefits and voting entitlements passed through under the product terms"; holders "are not registered shareholders of Anthropic, and Anthropic has not sponsored, authorized or endorsed Securitize Stocks". Jump Trading supports liquidity, transactions settle in USDC, and trading "is initially available beyond traditional market hours, with plans to move toward 24/7 availability" ([Securitize](https://investors.securitize.io/news/news-details/2026/Securitize-Tokenizes-Anthropic-Stock-Onchain-on-First-Day-of-Public-Trading/default.aspx)). See [[comparisons/implementations-by-model|Implementations by model]].

## TSV APIs and CET infrastructure (13 Nov 2026)
A 13 Nov 2026 article on Securitize's Medium publication describes infrastructure and APIs that TSV operators can integrate under the [[concepts/innovation-exemption|Innovation Exemption]] ([Securitize](https://medium.com/securitize/the-tsv-stack-you-bring-execution-and-liquidity-we-bring-the-stock-b2cb64c744ed)). Current developer documentation is at [developers.securitize.io](https://developers.securitize.io) (retrieved 13 Nov 2026).

In that article, CET is used both as a custodial entitlement token — a [[concepts/glossary#security-entitlement|security entitlement]] to a real share held through a broker-dealer and custody structure — and, "at Securitize," as a convertible [[concepts/glossary#entitlement-token|entitlement token]], designed so a CET can later convert into an issuer-sponsored token while keeping the same onchain identity ([Securitize](https://medium.com/securitize/the-tsv-stack-you-bring-execution-and-liquidity-we-bring-the-stock-b2cb64c744ed)). The convertible structure was already described in the 8 Oct 2026 launch release above.

The article names three APIs. Current docs describe them as follows ([Securitize docs](https://developers.securitize.io)):
- **SecId API** (called SecID in the article): investor onboarding, screening, eligibility and wallet permissioning, including a hosted onboarding option. The docs are not labelled Beta. They say Securitize can rely on a platform's existing identity verification, subject to an agreement, and still screens every investor against sanctions and PEP lists ([Securitize docs](https://developers.securitize.io/secid-api/overview); [Securitize](https://medium.com/securitize/the-tsv-stack-you-bring-execution-and-liquidity-we-bring-the-stock-b2cb64c744ed)).
- **CET Underlying API**: authorized market makers buy and sell the underlying stocks or ETFs over an authenticated WebSocket, and Securitize mints or burns the corresponding CETs to match. The docs are not labelled Beta. The article says minting and burning happen through Securitize, outside the TSV, and that this separation matters under the Innovation Exemption, which restricts primary issuance activity on the venue ([Securitize docs](https://developers.securitize.io/cet-underlying-api/overview); [Securitize](https://medium.com/securitize/the-tsv-stack-you-bring-execution-and-liquidity-we-bring-the-stock-b2cb64c744ed)).
- **Corporate Actions API**: event-driven information on corporate actions, dates and terms, changes, and trading restrictions such as halts, with signed webhooks, replay and a sandbox of scripted scenarios. The current overview is labelled **Beta**: the API and its webhooks are in development and subject to change, and paths, fields, enumerations and event names can change before 1.0.0 ([Securitize docs](https://developers.securitize.io/corporate-actions-api/overview); [Securitize](https://medium.com/securitize/the-tsv-stack-you-bring-execution-and-liquidity-we-bring-the-stock-b2cb64c744ed)).

The same documentation site also has a **Stocks API**, for trading tokenized US-listed stocks, issuer-sponsored tokens and convertible entitlement tokens for investors onboarded through the SecId API. The current overview is labelled **Beta**: the endpoints are being opened to partners through the partner gateway, and paths, fields and errors can change before release. Production access requires a UI audit by Securitize Compliance of the live screens, including disclosures and market data (NBBO included) ([Securitize docs](https://developers.securitize.io/stocks-api/overview)).

On splits, the article says that on supported EVM deployments Securitize uses display [[concepts/glossary#multiplier|multipliers]] based on the ERC-8056 approach, and on [[concepts/glossary#solana|Solana]] it can use Scaled UI Amount, so the economic representation can change without liquidity providers withdrawing and recreating positions ([Securitize](https://medium.com/securitize/the-tsv-stack-you-bring-execution-and-liquidity-we-bring-the-stock-b2cb64c744ed)). Dividends and other entitlements, in the same article, are handled through Securitize's corporate-actions processing rather than described as reinvested into that multiplier.

The article says this is the mechanism Securitize is using to support the [[platforms-venues/okxice-tsv|OKXICE TSV]] launch, and the same infrastructure it is leveraging in its work supporting [[platforms-venues/formation-securities|Formation's TSV]], "enabling both stocks and ETFs to trade through the same CET infrastructure" ([Securitize](https://medium.com/securitize/the-tsv-stack-you-bring-execution-and-liquidity-we-bring-the-stock-b2cb64c744ed)). The OKX tokenizer role is already recorded from OKX's 11 Nov 2026 announcement above ([OKX](https://www.okx.com/en-us/learn/okxice-tsv-launch)). The stocks-and-ETFs wording is Securitize's description; Formation's official 9 Nov 2026 notice names only three Tokenized NMS Stocks for launch (FWDI, GLXY and EXOD) and does not mention ETFs ([[platforms-venues/formation-securities|Formation Securities]], [Formation notice](https://www.formationsecurities.com/notice.pdf)).

The article also says the exemption requires a public notice at least 30 calendar days before operations start, and that the SEC expects the venue to be operationally ready when that notice is published. The notice period is not the build window: the integration should be ready before filing ([Securitize](https://medium.com/securitize/the-tsv-stack-you-bring-execution-and-liquidity-we-bring-the-stock-b2cb64c744ed)). The services described are subject to regulatory requirements, agreements, eligibility criteria and technical availability, and TSV operators remain responsible for the exemption conditions ([Securitize](https://medium.com/securitize/the-tsv-stack-you-bring-execution-and-liquidity-we-bring-the-stock-b2cb64c744ed)).

On 12 Nov 2026 Securitize filed a comment letter on the Innovation Exemption (File 4-927). It supports testing AMM-based TSVs and says it does not itself need the relief, because it already trades tokenized securities onchain under existing rules. The letter asks the SEC to require permissioning in the token itself rather than only in a pool allowlist, with freeze, burn and reissue as the fallback; to explicitly permit PropAMMs; to define "permissionless" with objective criteria; and to let issuers waive the 30-day wait by affirmative consent ([Securitize letter](https://www.sec.gov/comments/S7-2026-32/4927-1134705-4042498.pdf)). That is advocacy, not a change to the order; see [[concepts/innovation-exemption|Innovation Exemption]].

## What this means for holders
- Securitize Stocks are UCC Article 8 security entitlements backed 1:1, held through Securitize's broker-dealer, on Solana and settled in USDC ([PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/securitize-launches-global-onchain-trading-of-us-stocks-with-security-entitlements-302902107.html)).
- Where available, holders can convert to direct registration, i.e. hold the share on the company's register ([PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/securitize-launches-global-onchain-trading-of-us-stocks-with-security-entitlements-302902107.html)).
- Eligible investors in the US, EU and other permitted jurisdictions, subject to local rules ([PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/securitize-launches-global-onchain-trading-of-us-stocks-with-security-entitlements-302902107.html)).
- The shares backing the tokens are not lent out ([PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/securitize-launches-global-onchain-trading-of-us-stocks-with-security-entitlements-302902107.html)).

## DTC direct-registration comment letter (25 Oct 2026)
Securitize's issuer-sponsored tokens (ISTs) record the investor, not [[concepts/glossary#cede--co|Cede & Co.]], as the registered holder on the issuer's books ([Securitize letter](https://www.sec.gov/comments/SR-DTC-2026-012/srdtc2026012-1108719-3924407.pdf)). To turn an existing brokerage position into an IST, the shares must first leave [[concepts/glossary#dtc|DTC]] through the [[concepts/glossary#direct-registration-system-drs|Direct Registration System (DRS)]], which today can take several days ([Securitize letter](https://www.sec.gov/comments/SR-DTC-2026-012/srdtc2026012-1108719-3924407.pdf)). Securitize supports DTC's proposal to cut DRS processing "from days to minutes" ([SEC notice](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/10/05/2026-20301/self-regulatory-organizations-the-depository-trust-company-notice-of-filing-of-proposed-rule-change)) but asked the SEC for more ([Securitize letter](https://www.sec.gov/comments/SR-DTC-2026-012/srdtc2026012-1108719-3924407.pdf)):
- One instruction that burns a DTC-tokenized position and starts the DRS withdrawal, with a real-time completion message to the transfer agent ("Instant Burn-and-Notify").
- Published DRS processing targets and operating hours, reporting of actual times by stage, and the same modernization for [[concepts/glossary#dwac|DWAC]].
- A timing expectation for brokers to submit customer withdrawal requests, or letting the transfer agent start one on the investor's documented authorization.
- An equally fast path back to [[concepts/glossary#street-name|street name]].
- The tokenization link live by the commercial launch of DTCC's tokenization service, or a published timeline or follow-on filing within three months.

See [[infrastructure/dtcc-tokenization-pilot|DTCC / DTC tokenization pilot]]. Context: Brett Redfearn's [LinkedIn post](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/time-dtc-modernization-now-brett-w-redfearn-a3tse) (28 Oct 2026, opinion).
