*In plain English: Crypto.com sells tokens that follow US stock prices, but they are contracts, not shares.*

Crypto.com launched Tokenized Stocks on 12 Aug 2026 with exposure to 1,500 US stocks and ETFs from US\$1, traded 24/7 in the Crypto.com App ([Crypto.com](https://crypto.com/us/company-news/tokenized-stocks-eea-launch)).

## What this means for holders
- These are derivative contracts issued by Foris Capital CY Limited: no legal or beneficial ownership, no voting or shareholder rights ([Crypto.com](https://crypto.com/us/company-news/tokenized-stocks-eea-launch)).
- Dividend-equivalent adjustments may apply but are not guaranteed; hedging assets sit with Alpaca ([Crypto.com](https://crypto.com/us/company-news/tokenized-stocks-eea-launch)).
