*In plain English: bStocks are certificates backed one-for-one by real US shares held at a custodian.*

bStocks are certificates issued by BTECH Holdings Ltd, each backed 1:1 by a US share at a regulated [[concepts/glossary#custodian|custodian]], admitted to the FSRA Official List ([ADGM admission notice, 11 Jun 2026](https://www.adgm.com/globalassets/market-disclosure/content/anouncement/admission-of-securities-to-the-official-list--btech-holdings-limited_06_12_2026_18-11-40.pdf)). Each ticker has its own FSRA-approved prospectus, e.g. [NVIDIA bStocks, 11 Jun 2026](https://www.adgm.com/globalassets/market-disclosure/content/prospectus/btech-holdings-prospectus----11-june---nvidia-bstocks_06_11_2026_16-44-54.pdf).

## What this means for holders
- bStocks are certificates from BTECH Holdings backed 1:1, admitted to the ADGM FSRA Official List ([ADGM prospectus](https://www.adgm.com/globalassets/market-disclosure/content/prospectus/btech-holdings-prospectus----11-june---nvidia-bstocks_06_11_2026_16-44-54.pdf)).
- The offer is made only in ADGM and not to US persons ([ADGM prospectus](https://www.adgm.com/globalassets/market-disclosure/content/prospectus/btech-holdings-prospectus----11-june---nvidia-bstocks_06_11_2026_16-44-54.pdf)).
- Cash dividends received on the underlying are reinvested into additional shares (net of a 30% US withholding tax applicable to non-US holders) and reflected through an adjustment to the Multiplier; holders do not receive cash dividends directly ([ADGM prospectus](https://www.adgm.com/globalassets/market-disclosure/content/prospectus/btech-holdings-prospectus----11-june---nvidia-bstocks_06_11_2026_16-44-54.pdf)).

## Anthropic (ANTB) on Binance (19 Nov 2026)
Binance listed the bStocks Anthropic token, ANTB, on Binance Spot (ANTB/USDT) from 18:00 UTC on 19 Nov 2026, with deposits and withdrawals from 19:00 UTC on BNB Smart Chain (contract 0x38Dd26788Cf2d901E3aBf04efE3007d459d0Fc53). Holders of the stock directly can tokenize it into bStocks: "Conversion is 1:1 between each underlying stock and its bStocks, with zero conversion fees." ANTB was also added to Binance's supported assets for third-party conversion, so users "can deposit and convert eligible third-party tokenized securities into ANTB at a fixed 1:1 rate with zero fees for a limited time, subject to a daily promotional cap." Binance repeats that bStocks "are not stocks or shares", are not offered to US persons, and have no public offering outside the ADGM ([Binance](https://www.binance.com/en/support/announcement/detail/830c320848af4257812d5b6e99e031f9)). From the same time, ANTB counts as collateral for Binance Cross Margin, Portfolio Margin and Portfolio Margin Pro; borrowing ANTB is not supported ([Binance](https://www.binance.com/en/support/announcement/detail/c4a6afb79b32434a88e826b9632242c0)).

Binance Wallet also delivered ANTB to participants in its Anthropic IPO campaign: "roughly 2,957 Anthropic tokenized stocks (ANTB)" pro rata at no cost, plus a \$1,000,000 ANTB airdrop, after returning all committed USDC ([Binance](https://www.binance.com/en/support/announcement/detail/f37d1230c2e542fbb1872fcde3ebf140); [Binance Wallet](https://t.me/binance_wallet_announcements/1955)). ADGM's Official List shows "Anthropic bStocks" from BTech Holdings Limited as Listed: Certificates over Shares, ISIN AE000A4AXWN5, on Nest Exchange Limited ([ADGM Official List](https://www.adgm.com/financial-services-regulatory-authority/listing-authority/official-list-of-securities), retrieved 21 Nov 2026). An ANTB-specific prospectus was still not found; the list does not link one, and Binance's announcements link only to its general prospectus page ([Binance](https://www.binance.com/en/support/announcement/detail/830c320848af4257812d5b6e99e031f9)).
