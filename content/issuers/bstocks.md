*In plain English: bStocks are certificates backed one-for-one by real US shares held at a custodian.*

bStocks are certificates issued by BTECH Holdings Ltd, each backed 1:1 by a US share at a regulated [[concepts/glossary#custodian|custodian]], admitted to the FSRA Official List ([ADGM admission notice, 11 Jun 2026](https://www.adgm.com/globalassets/market-disclosure/content/anouncement/admission-of-securities-to-the-official-list--btech-holdings-limited_06_12_2026_18-11-40.pdf)). Each ticker has its own FSRA-approved prospectus, e.g. [NVIDIA bStocks, 11 Jun 2026](https://www.adgm.com/globalassets/market-disclosure/content/prospectus/btech-holdings-prospectus----11-june---nvidia-bstocks_06_11_2026_16-44-54.pdf).

## What this means for holders
- bStocks are certificates from BTECH Holdings backed 1:1, admitted to the ADGM FSRA Official List ([ADGM prospectus](https://www.adgm.com/globalassets/market-disclosure/content/prospectus/btech-holdings-prospectus----11-june---nvidia-bstocks_06_11_2026_16-44-54.pdf)).
- The offer is made only in ADGM and not to US persons ([ADGM prospectus](https://www.adgm.com/globalassets/market-disclosure/content/prospectus/btech-holdings-prospectus----11-june---nvidia-bstocks_06_11_2026_16-44-54.pdf)).
- Cash dividends received on the underlying are reinvested into additional shares (net of a 30% US withholding tax applicable to non-US holders) and reflected through an adjustment to the Multiplier; holders do not receive cash dividends directly ([ADGM prospectus](https://www.adgm.com/globalassets/market-disclosure/content/prospectus/btech-holdings-prospectus----11-june---nvidia-bstocks_06_11_2026_16-44-54.pdf)).
