On 4 Aug 2026 Dinari launched 724 dShares, including the full S&P 500, to eligible US investors ([Dinari](https://dinari.com/blog/dinari-launches-724-tokenized-stocks-available-to-u-s-investors-and-businesses)). Dinari Inc. is the transfer agent; Dinari Securities LLC is the broker-dealer ([GlobeNewswire](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/08/04/3338495/0/en/dinari-inc-opens-platform-licenses-to-u-s-financial-institutions.html)).

In its input to the SEC Crypto Task Force, Dinari describes tokens as secondary, non-transferable records mirroring authoritative off-chain brokerage books ([SEC](https://www.sec.gov/files/ctf-written-input-dinari-inc-040226.pdf)). US clearing is through Alpaca Securities LLC ([Dinari](https://docs.dinari.com/docs/us)). See [What is a dShare](https://docs.dinari.com/docs/what-is-dshare).
