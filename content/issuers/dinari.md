*In plain English: Dinari issues tokens backed by US stocks and offers them to eligible US investors.*

On 4 Aug 2026 Dinari launched 724 dShares, including the full S&P 500, to eligible US investors ([Dinari](https://dinari.com/blog/dinari-launches-724-tokenized-stocks-available-to-u-s-investors-and-businesses)). Dinari Inc. is the [[concepts/glossary#transfer-agent|transfer agent]]; Dinari Securities LLC is the [[concepts/glossary#broker-dealer|broker-dealer]] ([GlobeNewswire](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/08/04/3338495/0/en/dinari-inc-opens-platform-licenses-to-u-s-financial-institutions.html)).

In its input to the SEC Crypto Task Force, Dinari describes tokens as secondary, non-transferable records mirroring authoritative off-chain brokerage books ([SEC](https://www.sec.gov/files/ctf-written-input-dinari-inc-040226.pdf)). US clearing is through Alpaca Securities LLC ([Dinari](https://docs.dinari.com/docs/us)). See [What is a dShare](https://docs.dinari.com/docs/what-is-dshare).

## Which chains are dShares on?
Dinari's docs list supported chains as Ethereum, Arbitrum One, Avalanche C-Chain, Base, HyperEVM, HyperCore and Plume ([Dinari docs](https://docs.dinari.com/docs/blockchain)). In Aug 2025 Dinari launched the Dinari Financial Network, a Layer 1 on Avalanche that acts as an omni-chain order book and coordination layer for its partners' liquidity and settlement, governed by a consortium with Gemini, BitGo and VanEck as initial validators ([Dinari](https://dinari.com/blog/dinari-launches-the-dinari-financial-network-an-omni-chain-orderbook-powered-by-avalanche)). See [[build/trading-reference-architecture#how-venues-match-and-settle|How venues match and settle]].

## Can dShare holders vote?
Broadridge and Dinari announced that eligible dShare holders can vote the underlying shares through Broadridge's proxy infrastructure; votes form part of the formal shareholder vote and are counted with traditional shareholders, and holders receive company disclosures ([Broadridge](https://www.broadridge.com/press-release/2026/broadridge-brings-proxy-voting-to-dinaris-dshares-tokenized-us-equities)). Wallet login to prove entitlements and vote directly is a future step, not live. See [[infrastructure/broadridge|Broadridge]].

## Anthropic IPO (legal model)
Dinari said on 6 Nov 2026 that ANT will go live as a dShare the day it starts trading, for eligible investors in the US and 90+ countries ([Dinari on X](https://x.com/DinariGlobal/status/2118853664232374570)). Its IPO FAQ calls ANT dShares "Dinari's tokenized representation of Anthropic stock", "backed by the underlying security", says only whole-share limit orders are accepted before the opening trade, and says "Direct IPO purchase is not available through Dinari": participation is through the secondary market once the stock trades ([Dinari FAQ](https://docs.dinari.com/docs/anthropic-ipo-faqs)).

In the US a dShare is "a secondary ledger for real ownership in the underlying securities" held through Dinari Securities, and issued tokens are currently non-transferable (docs last updated 22 Jun 2026; [Dinari docs](https://docs.dinari.com/docs/us)); it is not an issuer-sponsored Anthropic share. Dinari's 17 Nov 2026 blog says its model preserves dividends, corporate actions and "voting rights" ([Dinari blog](https://dinari.com/blog/anthropic-is-coming-to-dshares)); that is Dinari's own claim, and no ANT-specific legal document was found to confirm it.

## What this means for holders
- You can sell or burn dShares for payment tokens (stablecoin proceeds); the custodian sells the share. No in-kind delivery of the share was found in issuer docs ([placing orders](https://docs.dinari.com/docs/placing-orders); [dShares](https://dinari.com/dshares)).
- Eligible holders can vote the underlying shares through Broadridge's proxy process ([Broadridge](https://www.broadridge.com/press-release/2026/broadridge-brings-proxy-voting-to-dinaris-dshares-tokenized-us-equities)).
- Eligible US investors can buy dShares since 4 Aug 2026 ([Dinari](https://dinari.com/blog/dinari-launches-724-tokenized-stocks-available-to-u-s-investors-and-businesses)).
- Dinari describes its tokens as records that mirror its brokerage books, so your claim runs through Dinari as [[concepts/glossary#broker-dealer|broker-dealer]] ([Dinari SEC input](https://www.sec.gov/files/ctf-written-input-dinari-inc-040226.pdf)).
- Dividends are paid in cash, with [[concepts/glossary#stablecoin|USDC]] support ([Dinari](https://dinari.com/blog/dinari-launches-724-tokenized-stocks-available-to-u-s-investors-and-businesses)).
