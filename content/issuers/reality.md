*In plain English: Bitget says rANT is a token, issued by Reality, that tracks Anthropic stock 1:1. It is not the share itself, and Bitget says it does not automatically give you shareholder status or a vote.*

Reality is the issuer Bitget names for rANT, the Anthropic tokenized stock offered through Bitget's IPO Prime and listed on Bitget's spot market ([Bitget IPO Prime](https://www.bitget.com/spotlight/ipo-prime); [Bitget](https://www.bitget.com/support/articles/12560603901016); [Bitget rANT page](https://www.bitget.com/price/anthropic-tokenized-stock-reality)). Bitget describes Reality as "a licensed RWA protocol." The retrieved pages do not give a legal entity name, jurisdiction or licence. A meta description on the rANT page also says "Reality Protocol."

## What does the holder have?

Bitget's rANT page says rANT "tracks the underlying ANT stock on a 1:1 basis" and "provides economic exposure to Anthropic stock, but holding rANT does not constitute direct ownership of Anthropic shares or confer shareholder voting rights" ([Bitget rANT page](https://www.bitget.com/price/anthropic-tokenized-stock-reality)). The 3 Nov IPO Prime announcement uses the same idea: "Holding rANT is not equivalent to directly holding Anthropic shares, and it does not automatically confer shareholder status, voting rights, or other rights. Specific rights, asset backing, and redemption arrangements are subject to the product documents" ([Bitget](https://www.bitget.com/support/articles/12560603899381)). Those product documents were not retrieved.

Bitget's wording on backing is not identical across pages. The IPO Prime page says rANT is "issued by Reality, which custodies the underlying Anthropic shares" and that each token "maps 1:1 to a share and represents the holder's on-chain beneficial interest" ([Bitget IPO Prime](https://www.bitget.com/spotlight/ipo-prime)). The rANT page says "each tokenized stock is backed 1:1 by real shares held in custody with a licensed broker" and that Reality has a "partnership with compliant broker Alpaca" ([Bitget rANT page](https://www.bitget.com/price/anthropic-tokenized-stock-reality)). This page records both claims as Bitget's; it does not treat them as a single legal structure, and Alpaca has not been cited here in its own words.

The chain and contract address were not stated in the retrieved articles.

## Anthropic (rANT) and IPO Prime (19 Nov 2026)

Bitget announced on 3 Nov 2026 (10:00 UTC+8; 9:00 PM ET on 2 Nov) that the Anthropic IPO subscription was coming to IPO Prime, and that the asset delivered would be rANT ([Bitget](https://www.bitget.com/support/articles/12560603899381)). The same announcement says the product "is not issued, offered, or managed by Anthropic," "has no official partnership or authorization relationship with Anthropic and has not been approved, sponsored, or endorsed by Anthropic."

A later announcement, timestamped 19 Nov 2026 06:59 UTC+8 (5:59 PM ET on 18 Nov), says Bitget "will list the stock spot asset rANT on November 19, 2026, with trading opening during US regular market hours" ([Bitget](https://www.bitget.com/support/articles/12560603901016)). The 3 Nov announcement had said trading was "expected to open about one hour after the underlying stock completes its Nasdaq IPO Cross (opening auction)," subject to change. The retrieved articles do not confirm the first trade time.

On 19 Nov 2026 at 17:10 UTC+8 (4:10 AM ET), Bitget said it had "received a final allocation of 153,774 Anthropic shares": 137,494 in the VIP round at an 11.26% allocation rate, and 16,280 in the public round at 1.36%, with public-round distribution and refunds by 19 Nov 20:00 UTC (3:00 PM ET) and VIP by 20 Nov 16:00 UTC (11:00 AM ET), per Bitget ([Bitget](https://www.bitget.com/support/articles/12560603901105)). Those figures are Bitget's. The allocation article names "Anthropic shares" and IPO Prime; it does not name rANT or Reality.

## What this means for holders
- Bitget says rANT is not the same as holding Anthropic shares and does not automatically confer shareholder status or voting rights ([Bitget](https://www.bitget.com/support/articles/12560603899381); [Bitget rANT page](https://www.bitget.com/price/anthropic-tokenized-stock-reality)).
- The product is available only to eligible users in permitted regions and is not offered to residents of restricted jurisdictions; the retrieved articles do not say whether US persons are included ([Bitget](https://www.bitget.com/support/articles/12560603899381)).
- Redemption and other rights are "subject to the product documents," which were not retrieved ([Bitget](https://www.bitget.com/support/articles/12560603899381)).
