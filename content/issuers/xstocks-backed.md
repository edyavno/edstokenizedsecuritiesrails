*In plain English: xStocks are tokens backed one-for-one by US stocks and ETFs.*

xStocks are stock and ETF tokens issued by Backed Assets (JE) Limited, backed 1:1 by the underlying asset. They launched on Bybit, Kraken and [[concepts/glossary#solana|Solana]] DeFi on 30 June 2025 ([PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/backeds-xstocks-go-live-today-on-bybit-kraken-and-solana-defi-302494374.html)).

On 2 December 2025 Kraken announced it **agreed to acquire** Backed Finance AG ([Kraken](https://blog.kraken.com/news/backed-acquisition); [Business Wire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20251202666578/en/Kraken-to-Acquire-Backed-Accelerating-xStocks-Expansion-and-Unlocking-Global-Demand-for-Tokenized-Equities)); completion status not confirmed here.

At that date xStocks offered more than 60 assets, live on Solana and [[concepts/glossary#ethereum|Ethereum]]. xStocks are not available in the US ([Kraken press release](https://www.payward.com/press-release/kraken-acquires-backed-xstocks-expansion)). Kraken's xStocks FAQ now lists 131 assets (100 stocks, 27 ETFs, 4 specialist assets) on Solana, Ethereum, TON and Ink, for eligible non-US clients in 110+ countries; not the US, Canada, the UK or Australia ([Kraken FAQ](https://support.kraken.com/articles/xstocks-faq)).

A figure of 1,100 assets advertised elsewhere ([Zerion](https://zerion.io/blog/best-tokenized-stock-platforms/), secondary) is unverified and not used.

Corporate actions use a [[concepts/glossary#multiplier|multiplier]] ([xStocks docs](https://docs.xstocks.fi/developers/multipliers)): dividends are reinvested net of withholding and raise the multiplier ([xStocks docs](https://docs.xstocks.fi/docs/dividends-and-stock-splits)); holders get no voting rights ([Kraken FAQ](https://support.kraken.com/articles/xstocks-faq)); see [[topics/corporate-actions-and-dividends|corporate actions]]; exchange integration and API: [integration](https://docs.xstocks.fi/docs/exchange-integration), [OpenAPI](https://docs.xstocks.fi/apis/openapi.md).

## What this means for holders
- You hold a 1:1-backed [[concepts/glossary#wrapper|wrapper]] certificate issued by Backed Assets (Jersey), not the share itself ([Kraken FAQ](https://support.kraken.com/articles/xstocks-faq)).
- Dividends and splits are reflected by adjusting the token's [[concepts/glossary#multiplier|multiplier]] ([xStocks docs](https://docs.xstocks.fi/docs/dividends-and-stock-splits)).
- Not offered in the US, Canada, the UK or Australia ([Kraken FAQ](https://support.kraken.com/articles/xstocks-faq)).
- Tokens can be withdrawn to a self-custody [[concepts/glossary#wallet|wallet]] ([PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/backeds-xstocks-go-live-today-on-bybit-kraken-and-solana-defi-302494374.html)).
