*In plain English: xStocks are tokens, issued by Backed, that are backed one-for-one by listed stocks and ETFs, now including a first batch of UK and EU names.*

xStocks are stock and ETF tokens issued by Backed Assets (JE) Limited, backed 1:1 by the underlying asset. They launched on Bybit, Kraken and [[concepts/glossary#solana|Solana]] DeFi on 30 June 2025 ([PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/backeds-xstocks-go-live-today-on-bybit-kraken-and-solana-defi-302494374.html)).

On 2 December 2025 Kraken announced it **agreed to acquire** Backed Finance AG ([Kraken](https://blog.kraken.com/news/backed-acquisition); [Business Wire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20251202666578/en/Kraken-to-Acquire-Backed-Accelerating-xStocks-Expansion-and-Unlocking-Global-Demand-for-Tokenized-Equities)); completion status not confirmed here.

At that date xStocks offered more than 60 assets, live on Solana and [[concepts/glossary#ethereum|Ethereum]]. xStocks are not available in the US ([Kraken press release](https://www.payward.com/press-release/kraken-acquires-backed-xstocks-expansion)). Kraken's xStocks FAQ listed 131 assets (100 stocks, 27 ETFs, 4 specialist assets) on Solana, Ethereum, TON and Ink as of 25 Oct 2026, for eligible non-US clients in 110+ countries; not the US, Canada, the UK or Australia ([Kraken FAQ](https://support.kraken.com/articles/xstocks-faq)).

On 11 Nov 2026 a Payward press release said xStocks had deployed its first batch of tokenized equities representing UK and European publicly listed assets, a first wave of 200+ companies listed in the UK and EU, with further listings to be added on a rolling basis. The release does not name the individual UK or EU issuers. It says the new tokens are available to trade across Kraken and supporting xStocks Alliance platforms from day one, and in the DeFi ecosystems where xStocks already trade, including Solana, Ethereum, TON, Ink, X Layer and other chains. Payward reports that this launch takes xStocks past 1,500 tokenized assets in total, with over $1.1 billion in assets under management, total transaction volume past $45 billion, and more than 550,000 unique holders; those figures are company-reported ([Payward](https://www.payward.com/press-release/xstocks-uk-eu-equities), raw/243). The $45 billion cumulative mark was first stated in a 28 Oct 2026 Payward release ([Payward, 28 Oct](https://www.payward.com/press-release/xstocks-surpasses-1-billion-aum), raw/246). Dated volume figures, and earlier company marks, are in [[comparisons/reported-trading-volume-by-venue|Reported trading volume by venue]]. A figure of 1,100 assets advertised earlier elsewhere ([Zerion](https://zerion.io/blog/best-tokenized-stock-platforms/), secondary) is unverified and is not used.

The same release describes the UK and EU names as the first expansion markets under Payward Services' partnership with GTN, "which handles global execution and custody of the traditional assets that underpin each token." That is Payward's description of the partnership; this page does not treat it as an independently confirmed custody chart for every historic xStock, and earlier issuer docs did not name a custodian. The release also says each issued token is matched by a purchase of the underlying share "held at a regulated custodian," without naming the firm in that sentence ([Payward](https://www.payward.com/press-release/xstocks-uk-eu-equities)). Payward says hundreds of Hong Kong-listed equities will follow in the coming weeks, and that in 2027, subject to regulatory approval, the London Stock Exchange intends to list xStocks and start trading them on LSE 24. Distribution and eligibility are unchanged, and the tokens remain unavailable to investors in the United Kingdom and are not available in the U.S. or to U.S. persons ([Payward](https://www.payward.com/press-release/xstocks-uk-eu-equities)).

A separate Payward release, also dated 11 Nov 2026, says xStocks has deployed tokenized equities representing every constituent of the DAX 40, as part of that UK and EU expansion. The DAX 40 tokens "will be immediately available on 360X, a company of Deutsche Börse Group, as well as Xetra-Gold as xStocks." Named examples available from day one include SAP (SAPx), Siemens (SIEx), Allianz (ALV.DEx) and Deutsche Telekom (DTE.DEx). The same release describes 360X as "the Deutsche Börse-backed venue regulated under the EU's DLT Pilot Regime since February 2026," and says xStocks already trade there; that is Payward's description of the venue, not a claim that the DAX 40 admission itself is a new Pilot Regime authorisation ([Payward](https://www.payward.com/press-release/xstocks-dax-40), raw/299). Catalog figures in this DAX 40 release, company-reported as of 11 Nov 2026, are "more than 1,300 xStocks," "over \$1 billion" in assets under management, "more than \$45 billion of total transaction volume," and "more than 500,000 unique holders." Those counts differ from the same-day UK/EU release (raw/243) and should be quoted with this date, not flattened into one current number.

On 12 Nov 2026 the Optimism Foundation, in a joint announcement with xStocks and ether.fi, said xStocks' full catalog of 1,500+ tokenized equities has been natively minted and redeemed on OP Mainnet for several months, with no OFT bridge in the path, and that the teams were confirming that publicly that day. The post says the catalog covers the full Russell 1,000 plus examples such as NVDAx, TSLAx, QQQx, SPYx and TBLLx. ether.fi, which had offered wrapped xStocks in a summer 2026 release, went live with native mint and redeem in-app that day. The post also says xStocks is deployed on 12 chains and cites Dune for the 1,500+ catalog count and for "\$46B+" total transaction volume since June 2025; it does not list the 12 chains ([Optimism](https://optimism.io/blog/xstocks-native-op-mainnet-ether-fi-launch), raw/298). Quote those figures with this 12 Nov 2026 date. The post also says xStocks are not available in the US, the UK, or other restricted jurisdictions.

Separately, Kraken's xStocks FAQ, last updated 11 Nov 2026, says underlying shares are held by Alpaca Securities LLC (FINRA-regulated, SIPC member), with supplemental coverage by Lloyd's of London up to $175 million aggregate, and names InCore Bank as a secondary custodian. It also says the SPV structure is designed so that if Kraken or Backed goes bankrupt, users can still claim the underlying value of their assets directly with Alpaca. That is Kraken's FAQ wording. It is not in the official xStocks issuer FAQ, which still refers to "regulated custodians" without naming them ([Kraken FAQ](https://support.kraken.com/articles/xstocks-faq), raw/261; [xStocks FAQ](https://docs.xstocks.fi/docs/frequently-asked-questions), raw/260). These company statements are recorded side by side, not reconciled here.


Corporate actions use a [[concepts/glossary#multiplier|multiplier]] ([xStocks docs](https://docs.xstocks.fi/developers/multipliers)): dividends are reinvested net of withholding and raise the multiplier ([xStocks docs](https://docs.xstocks.fi/docs/dividends-and-stock-splits)); holders get no voting rights ([Kraken FAQ](https://support.kraken.com/articles/xstocks-faq)); see [[topics/corporate-actions-and-dividends|corporate actions]]; exchange integration and API: [integration](https://docs.xstocks.fi/docs/exchange-integration), [OpenAPI](https://docs.xstocks.fi/apis/openapi.md).

## Primary market and trading routes
"xstocks" is one of the issuer values in Alpaca's Instant Tokenization Network mint API, in which an authorized participant's shares move to the issuer's Alpaca account before tokens are minted to the AP's wallet ([Alpaca mint API](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/reference/posttokenizationmintbroker); [[build/trading-reference-architecture#primary-market-mint-and-redeem-broker-custodied-model|mint and redeem, step by step]]). That shows xStocks is set up as an ITN issuer, not which custodian holds all backing shares. xStocks also quote into Jito Labs' JTX RFQ terminal on Solana ([SolanaFloor](https://solanafloor.com/news/jtx-equities-discoverability-rfq-execution-1650-tokenized-stocks), Jito-affiliated outlet).

**Anthropic IPO (legal model):** Kraken's IPO page offers Anthropic exposure "powered by xStocks" as ANTx, not available to US clients ([Kraken](https://www.kraken.com/ipo/anthropic), raw/206). That is a Backed tracker certificate, not an Anthropic share; Anthropic's amended S-1 does not mention tokenized shares ([S-1/A](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2130357/000162828026068676/anthropicpbc-sx1ax1.htm)).

## What this means for holders
- You hold a 1:1-backed [[concepts/glossary#wrapper|wrapper]] certificate issued by Backed Assets (Jersey), not the share itself ([Kraken FAQ](https://support.kraken.com/articles/xstocks-faq); [xStocks FAQ](https://docs.xstocks.fi/docs/frequently-asked-questions)).
- After KYC, you can redeem with the issuer for USDC, USDG or USDT ($5,000 minimum stated for direct issuer flow; fees currently 0) or, via xPort after Alpaca onboarding, for the underlying shares in an Alpaca brokerage account ([FAQ](https://docs.xstocks.fi/docs/frequently-asked-questions); [Market Flow](https://docs.xstocks.fi/docs/issuance-and-redemption/market-flow); [xPort](https://docs.xstocks.fi/docs/issuance-and-redemption/in-kind-flow-xport)). Most users exit on secondary markets.
- Dividends and splits are reflected by adjusting the token's [[concepts/glossary#multiplier|multiplier]] ([xStocks docs](https://docs.xstocks.fi/docs/dividends-and-stock-splits)).
- Not offered in the US, Canada, the UK or Australia ([Kraken FAQ](https://support.kraken.com/articles/xstocks-faq)).
- Tokens can be withdrawn to a self-custody [[concepts/glossary#wallet|wallet]] ([PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/backeds-xstocks-go-live-today-on-bybit-kraken-and-solana-defi-302494374.html)).
