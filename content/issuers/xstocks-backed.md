xStocks are stock and ETF tokens issued by Backed Assets (JE) Limited, backed 1:1 by the underlying asset. They launched on Bybit, Kraken and Solana DeFi on 30 June 2025 ([PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/backeds-xstocks-go-live-today-on-bybit-kraken-and-solana-defi-302494374.html)). On 2 December 2025 Kraken announced it **agreed to acquire** Backed Finance AG ([Kraken](https://blog.kraken.com/news/backed-acquisition); [Business Wire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20251202666578/en/Kraken-to-Acquire-Backed-Accelerating-xStocks-Expansion-and-Unlocking-Global-Demand-for-Tokenized-Equities)); completion status not confirmed here. At that date xStocks offered more than 60 assets, live on Solana and Ethereum. xStocks are not available in the US ([Kraken press release](https://www.payward.com/press-release/kraken-acquires-backed-xstocks-expansion)).

Corporate actions use a multiplier ([xStocks docs](https://docs.xstocks.fi/developers/multipliers)); exchange integration and API: [integration](https://docs.xstocks.fi/docs/exchange-integration), [OpenAPI](https://docs.xstocks.fi/apis/openapi.md).
